1 运动学图像：s-t 图与 v-t 图Kinematics graphs: s-t and v-t Kinematics运动学
学完你会：看懂一张运动图，能读出速度、加速度和位移。By the end: you can read velocity, acceleration and displacement straight off a motion graph.
说人话：两张图，看什么取决于你看的是斜率还是面积。s-t 图越陡表示走得越快（斜率就是速度）；v-t 图越陡表示加速越猛（斜率就是加速度），而它下面盖住的那块面积就是你走过的位移。这两个"斜率=什么、面积=什么"，是运动学的命根子。 In plain words: two graphs, and everything comes down to gradient or area. On an s-t graph, steeper means faster (the gradient is the velocity). On a v-t graph, steeper means harder acceleration (the gradient is the acceleration), and the area underneath is how far you travelled. "Gradient = what, area = what" is the whole game here.
记住这几点：Remember these:
  • s-t 图：斜率 = 速度。水平线 = 站着不动。s-t graph: gradient = velocity. A flat line means standing still.
  • v-t 图：斜率 = 加速度；面积 = 位移。v-t graph: gradient = acceleration; area = displacement.
  • v-t 图是水平线 → 匀速；是斜直线 → 匀加速。A flat v-t line means constant velocity; a sloping straight line means constant acceleration.
  • 曲线下面积不会算？就当梯形算：½(上底+下底)×高。Not sure about the area? Treat it as a trapezium: ½(a + b) × height.

例题Example

一个物体从静止开始做匀加速直线运动，5 s 后速度达到 10 m/s。求：(a) 加速度；(b) 这 5 s 内走的位移。An object starts from rest and accelerates uniformly, reaching 10 m/s after 5 s. Find (a) the acceleration and (b) the displacement in those 5 s.
(a) v-t 图是一条从 0 升到 10、横着走 5 格的直线。斜率 = 加速度：(a) The v-t line goes from 0 up to 10 across 5 seconds. Gradient = acceleration: a = (10 − 0) / 5 = 2 m/s² (b) 位移 = v-t 图下的面积，这里是个三角形：(b) Displacement = area under the v-t graph, which here is a triangle: s = ½ × 5 × 10 = 25 m （梯形算也一样：½(0 + 10) × 5 = 25 m）(The trapezium rule gives the same: ½(0 + 10) × 5 = 25 m.)

2 匀加速公式（SUVAT）The SUVAT equations SUVAT匀加速公式
学完你会：看到题目先列 u、v、a、s、t，选对公式一步算出来。By the end: you list u, v, a, s, t first, then pick the equation with only one unknown.
说人话：这五个字母，题目通常会给你三个，你要求的剩下一个。解题第一步永远是"列清单"：把已知的写下来，未知的打个问号，然后找一条公式，里面只有一个问号——就是它了。不用背四个公式谁是谁，看清单选就行。 In plain words: of these five letters, a question usually gives you three and asks for one more. Step one is always "write the list": jot down what you know, put a question mark by what you don't, then find the equation with only one question mark in it. No need to memorise which equation is which — let the list choose.
记住这几点：Remember these:
  • v = u + at  （不含 s）(no s)
  • s = ut + ½at²  （不含 v）(no v)
  • v² = u² + 2as  （不含 t，最常被忘）(no t — the one people forget)
  • s = ½(u + v)t  （不含 a）(no a)
  • 方向要统一：先定一个正方向，反方向的量加负号。Fix one direction as positive first, and give anything pointing the other way a minus sign.

列清单：u = 10，a = 2，t = 6，v = ?，s = ?List: u = 10, a = 2, t = 6, v = ?, s = ? 求 v：用 v = u + at（这条不含 s，正好）Find v using v = u + at (no s in it — perfect) v = 10 + 2 × 6 = 22 m/s 求 s：用 s = ut + ½at²Find s using s = ut + ½at² s = 10 × 6 + ½ × 2 × 36 = 60 + 36 = 96 m

3 自由落体与抛体运动Free fall and projectiles Projectile抛体
学完你会：把斜着扔的运动拆成"水平匀速 + 竖直匀加速"两半来算。By the end: you split any thrown motion into "constant horizontal + accelerating vertical".
说人话：扔出去的东西，水平方向没人管它（忽略空气阻力），所以一直匀速；竖直方向被重力拽着，所以是 a = g 的匀加速。两个方向互不干扰，唯一把它们连起来的东西是时间——水平飞多远和竖直掉多深，花的是同一段时间。所以先算竖直方向求时间，再去算水平位移。 In plain words: for a thrown object, nothing acts horizontally (ignore air resistance), so it keeps a constant horizontal velocity. Gravity pulls vertically, so vertically it accelerates at g. The two never interfere, and the only thing linking them is time — the horizontal distance and the vertical drop happen over the same seconds. So: work out the time from the vertical motion, then use it for the horizontal.
记住这几点：Remember these:
  • 自由落体：u = 0，a = g = 9.8 m/s²，所有物体落地时间一样（不看质量）。Free fall: u = 0, a = g = 9.8 m/s². All objects take the same time regardless of mass.
  • 抛体：水平速度永远不变，竖直速度按 v = u + gt 变。Projectile: horizontal velocity never changes; vertical follows v = u + gt.
  • 往上抛到最高点：那一刻竖直速度为 0，但水平速度还在。At the top of the flight the vertical velocity is 0 — but the horizontal velocity is still there.
  • 落地时间只由高度决定，和水平扔多远无关。Time of flight depends only on the height, not on how hard you throw it sideways.

4 牛顿三定律Newton's laws of motion Newton's laws牛顿定律
学完你会：用 F = ma 算加速度，并能用英文解释牛顿第三定律。By the end: you can use F = ma, and explain Newton's third law in English.
说人话：三条定律其实就三句话。第一定律：没人推你，你就保持原样（不动的继续不动，动的继续匀速）。第二定律：F = ma，力越大、质量越小，加速越猛——注意这里的 F 是合力，要把所有力加起来（反方向的减掉）。第三定律：你推墙，墙也推你，两个力一样大、方向相反。 In plain words: the three laws are three sentences. First law: if nothing pushes you, you keep doing what you were doing (still stays still, moving keeps moving at constant velocity). Second law: F = ma — bigger force or smaller mass means harder acceleration, and the F here is the resultant force, so you must add all the forces up (subtracting any that point the other way). Third law: if you push a wall, the wall pushes you back with an equal and opposite force.
记住这几点：Remember these:
  • F = ma ：F 是合力（单位 N），m 是质量（kg），a 是加速度（m/s²）。: F is the resultant force (N), m the mass (kg), a the acceleration (m/s²).
  • 重量 W = mg，和质量不是一回事（质量到哪儿都一样，重量会变）。Weight W = mg, which is not the same as mass (mass is the same everywhere; weight changes).
  • 第三定律的两个力：大小相等、方向相反、作用在不同物体上。Third-law pair: equal in size, opposite in direction, acting on different objects.
🗣 考场英文怎么说（第三定律，几乎每年都考）：🗣 How to say it in the exam (third law — it comes up almost every year): The forces are equal in magnitude and opposite in direction, and they act on different bodies. 注意：一定要说 act on different bodies（作用在不同物体上），只说"相等相反"会扣分——因为作用在同一物体上的相等相反的力叫平衡力，不是第三定律。 Note: you must say act on different bodies. Saying only "equal and opposite" loses marks — two equal and opposite forces on the same body are balanced forces, not a third-law pair.

5 动量与碰撞Momentum and collisions Momentum动量
学完你会：用「碰前总动量 = 碰后总动量」解碰撞题。By the end: you solve collisions using "total momentum before = total momentum after".
说人话：动量 p = mv，可以理解成"这东西有多难停下来"——大卡车慢慢开也很难拦住。只要没有外力，碰撞前后的总动量一点都不会少。注意：动量有方向，向左的动量要带负号，这是最容易错的地方。 In plain words: momentum p = mv, which you can think of as "how hard is this thing to stop" — a lorry moving slowly is still hard to stop. As long as there is no external force, the total momentum before and after a collision is exactly the same. Note that momentum has a direction, so momentum to the left gets a minus sign — that is the classic mistake.
记住这几点：Remember these:
  • p = mv ，单位 kg·m/s（或 N·s）。, unit kg·m/s (or N·s).
  • 动量守恒：m₁u₁ + m₂u₂ = m₁v₁ + m₂v₂Conservation: m₁u₁ + m₂u₂ = m₁v₁ + m₂v₂
  • 冲量 = 力 × 时间 = 动量的变化量（Ft = mv − mu）。Impulse = force × time = the change in momentum (Ft = mv − mu).
  • 三种碰撞：弹性（动量 + 动能都守恒）、非弹性（动量守恒，动能损失）、完全非弹性（撞完粘一起）。Three types: elastic (momentum and kinetic energy both conserved), inelastic (momentum conserved, energy lost), perfectly inelastic (they stick together).

6 功、能量与功率Work, energy and power Work & energy功与能
学完你会：算功、动能、势能，并用能量守恒解"滑下来有多快"这类题。By the end: you can calculate work, kinetic and potential energy, and use conservation of energy for "how fast at the bottom" questions.
说人话：功 = 力 × 沿这个力方向移动的距离（推墙推不动，物理上做功为 0）。能量是"能干多少活"，动能是动着的能量，势能是"位置高"存着的能量。能量守恒就是说：高处存的那份，滑下来就变成了速度；如果有摩擦，就有一部分变成了热，不能算丢。 In plain words: work = force × distance moved along that force (push a wall and it doesn't move, so you have done zero work). Energy is "how much work you could do". Kinetic energy is the energy of moving; potential energy is energy stored by being high up. Conservation of energy means: what is stored up high turns into speed on the way down; if there is friction, some becomes heat and must not be forgotten.
记住这几点：Remember these:
  • W = Fs cos θ ，θ 是力与位移的夹角（同向时 cos θ = 1）。, where θ is the angle between the force and the displacement (cos θ = 1 when they are parallel).
  • Ek = ½mv²  ·  Ep = mgh
  • P = W/t = Fv ，单位 W（瓦）。, unit W (watts).
  • 效率 = 有用的输出 / 总输入 × 100%。Efficiency = useful output / total input × 100%.

7 受力分析与斜面Force diagrams and inclined planes Inclined plane斜面
学完你会：画受力图、分解力，算出斜面上的加速度。By the end: you can draw a force diagram, resolve forces, and find the acceleration on a slope.
说人话：斜面题的万能招是把重力劈成两半：一份沿着斜面（mg sinθ，让它往下滑），一份垂直压在斜面上（mg cosθ，被斜面顶住）。光滑斜面上只有沿斜面那一份在推它，所以 ma = mg sinθ，质量约掉了，加速度 a = g sinθ——不管是铁球还是木块，滑下来一样快。 In plain words: the universal move is to split the weight in two: one part along the slope (mg sinθ, which makes it slide) and one part pressing into the slope (mg cosθ, which the surface pushes back on). On a smooth slope only the along-the-slope part pulls it, so ma = mg sinθ. The mass cancels, giving a = g sinθ — an iron ball and a wooden block slide down at the same rate.
记住这几点：Remember these:
  • 受力图五步：选物体 → 画重力 → 画接触力（支持力/摩擦垂直或平行接触面）→ 画绳的拉力 → 检查合力。Five steps for a force diagram: pick the body → draw weight → draw contact forces (normal and friction) → draw any tension → check the resultant.
  • 沿斜面：mg sinθ；垂直斜面：mg cosθ（θ 是斜面与水平面的夹角）。Along: mg sinθ; into the slope: mg cosθ (θ is the angle to the horizontal).
  • 光滑斜面 a = g sinθ；有摩擦再减去 μ g cosθ。Smooth slope: a = g sinθ. With friction, subtract μg cosθ.
  • 摩擦力 f ≤ μR，方向和相对运动相反。Friction f ≤ μR, and it opposes the relative motion.

2 力与形变：胡克定律Force and deformation: Hooke's law Hooke's law胡克定律
学完你会：用 F = kx 算弹簧伸长，并从图里读出 k。By the end: you can use F = kx and read the spring constant off a graph.
说人话：弹簧很"讲道理"：力加倍，伸长也加倍，这就是 F = kx。k 是这根弹簧的"硬度"，越难拉的 k 越大。但弹簧有脾气——拉过头就回不去了，那个临界点叫弹性极限，图上线一弯就是过了。 In plain words: a spring is very fair-minded: double the force, double the extension — that is F = kx. The constant k is how stiff the spring is; the harder it is to pull, the bigger k. But springs have a limit: pull too far and it will not go back. That point is the elastic limit, and it is where the graph starts to bend.
记住这几点：Remember these:
  • F = kx：F 是力（N），k 是劲度系数（N/m），x 是伸长量不是总长！: F in N, k in N/m, and x is the extension, not the total length!
  • F–x 图的斜率就是 k。On an F–x graph, the gradient is k.
  • 弹簧串联变软（k 变小），并联变硬（k 变大）。Springs in series get softer (smaller k); in parallel they get stiffer (bigger k).

3 弹性势能：拉长存了多少能Elastic potential energy Elastic energy弹性势能
学完你会：算弹簧里存了多少能量，并知道为什么公式里有个 ½。By the end: you can find the energy stored in a spring, and explain where the ½ comes from.
说人话：把弹簧拉长，你做的功就存在弹簧里，松手它就还给你。存了多少？看 F–x 图下面那块三角形——为什么是三角形而不是矩形？因为你一开始几乎不用力，越拉越费劲，平均下来只用了最大力的一半，所以有个 ½。 In plain words: stretching a spring stores the work you do inside the spring, and it hands it back when you let go. 学完你会：算应力和应变，知道为什么要用它们代替"力和伸长"。By the end: you can calculate stress and strain, and say why we use them instead of raw force and extension.
说人话：同样 100 N 的力，拉细铁丝能拉断，拉粗钢筋毫无反应——所以光看力不行，要看"分摊到每平方米上多少力"，这就是应力。同理，10 cm 伸长 1 cm 和 1 m 伸长 1 cm 完全不是一回事，所以要看比例，这就是应变。归一化了以后，同材料结果就一样。 In plain words: the same 100 N will snap a thin wire but barely bother a thick steel bar — so the force alone is not enough, we need the force per square metre, which is stress. 5 杨氏模量The Young modulus Young modulus杨氏模量
学完你会：用 E = FL/(Ax) 算杨氏模量，并知道它是材料的固有属性。By the end: you can use E = FL/(Ax) and explain that it is a property of the material alone.
说人话：杨氏模量就是问一句"这材料有多难拉伸"——数值越大越硬。它把力和尺寸都排除了，所以同一材料，做成粗的细的长的短的，E 都一样。这就是为什么工程师喜欢用它：查表就知道这块钢会怎么表现。 In plain words: the Young modulus answers "how hard is this material to stretch" — bigger means stiffer. It strips out the force and the size, so the same material has the same E whether it is thick, thin, long or short. That is why engineers like it: look it up in a table and you know how the steel will behave.
记住这几点：Remember these:
  • E = stress / strain = FL / (Ax)
  • 单位 Pa，但数值巨大，常用 GPa（1 GPa = 10⁹ Pa）。Unit Pa, but the numbers are huge, so GPa is usual (1 GPa = 10⁹ Pa).
  • 弹性范围内的应力–应变图斜率就是 E。Within the elastic region, the gradient of the stress–strain graph is E.
  • 和弹簧 k 的关系：k = EA / L（同样材料，越粗越硬、越长越软）。Link to the spring constant: k = EA / L (thicker is stiffer, longer is softer).

例题Example

E = FL / (Ax)，先统一单位：x = 2.0 mm = 2.0 × 10⁻³ mE = FL / (Ax), with x = 2.0 mm = 2.0 × 10⁻³ m E = (300 × 2.0) / (1.5 × 10⁻⁶ × 2.0 × 10⁻³) = 600 / (3.0 × 10⁻⁹) = 2.0 × 10¹¹ Pa 即 200 GPa，和钢的标准值吻合。That is 200 GPa, matching the standard value for steel.

6 应力–应变图：读材料的性格Stress–strain graphs: reading a material's personality Stress–strain应力应变图
学完你会：从图上认出弹性极限、极限强度、断裂点，并区分韧性与脆性。By the end: you can identify the elastic limit, UTS and breaking point, and tell ductile from brittle.
说人话：这张图就是材料的性格测试。开头直线段：老老实实，松手能弹回（弹性区）。过了弯点（弹性极限）：开始"记仇"，松手回不去了（塑性变形）。最高点是它能扛的极限，之后开始"脖子变细"然后断。铜这样的韧性材料会先拉长很长；玻璃这样的脆性材料一点不拉长，直接崩。 In plain words: this graph is a personality test for a material. The initial straight part: well behaved, it springs back when you let go (elastic region). Past the bend (elastic limit): it starts holding a grudge, and will not go back (plastic deformation). The peak is the most it can take, after which it starts to neck and then breaks. Ductile materials like copper stretch a long way first; brittle ones like glass barely stretch at all before snapping.
记住这几点（这几个词考卷上直接给分）：Remember these (these words are marks on the paper):
  • 弹性变形 elastic deformation：去掉力后能完全恢复。Elastic deformation: fully recovers when the load is removed.
  • 塑性变形 plastic deformation：去掉力后回不去，永久变形。Plastic deformation: does not recover — permanently changed.
  • 韧性 ductile：断裂前能拉很长（铜、低碳钢）。Ductile: stretches a lot before breaking (copper, mild steel).
  • 脆性 brittle：几乎不塑性变形就断（玻璃、陶瓷、铸铁）。Brittle: breaks with almost no plastic deformation (glass, ceramics, cast iron).

7 测杨氏模量的实验Measuring the Young modulus Experiment实验
学完你会：说出这个实验的器材、步骤和三条减小误差的办法。By the end: you can describe the apparatus, the method, and three ways to reduce error.
说人话：这个实验唯一的难点是：伸长量太小了，一根 2 m 的钢丝挂几公斤，可能只长 0.5 mm。所以整个实验的设计思路就两个字：放大。丝要长（伸长才明显）、直径要用螺旋测微器量准（A 差一点点，E 就差很多）、画图取斜率（比拿一个点硬算稳）。 In plain words: the one difficulty here is that the extension is tiny — a 2 m steel wire under a few kilograms may stretch only 0.5 mm. So the whole design comes down to one word: magnify. Use a long wire (bigger extension), measure the diameter with a micrometer (a small error in A throws E right off), and take the gradient of a graph (steadier than trusting a single reading).
记住这几点：Remember these:
  • 器材：长金属丝 + 砝码 + 螺旋测微器（量直径）+ 米尺（量长度）+ 游标/标尺（量伸长）。Apparatus: long wire + slotted masses + micrometer (diameter) + metre rule (length) + scale (extension).
  • 误差来源：直径的测量（因为要平方，影响最大）、读数视差、丝没拉直。Errors: the diameter (it gets squared, so it matters most), parallax when reading, and a wire that is not straight.
  • 改进：测直径多测几次取平均、加卸载荷都读数、画 F–x 图取斜率。Improvements: repeat and average the diameter, read on both loading and unloading, and take the gradient of an F–x graph.
  • 安全提示：戴护目镜，丝断了会弹回来。Safety: wear safety goggles — a snapping wire can fly back.
🗣 考场英文怎么说（实验改进题的高频句）：🗣 How to say it in the exam (high-frequency sentences for "improve the experiment"): Measure the diameter in several places and at different orientations, then take the mean. Plot a graph of force against extension and use the gradient, rather than a single pair of readings.

1 波的两个身份：λ 与 fA wave's two identities: λ and f Wavelength & frequency波长与频率
学完你会：看着波形读波长，用 v = fλ 做三类换算。By the end: you can read the wavelength off a waveform and use v = fλ in all three directions.
说人话：想象你站在海边看浪。波长 λ 是两个浪尖之间的距离；频率 f 是每秒有几个浪拍到你腿上。每秒过来 2 个浪、每个浪之间隔 3 米，那浪跑的速度就是 2 × 3 = 6 米每秒——这就是 v = fλ，不用背，理解了这个画面就记住了。 In plain words: picture yourself at the seashore. The wavelength λ is the gap between two crests; the frequency f is how many waves hit your legs each second. 2 横波与纵波Transverse and longitudinal waves Transverse / longitudinal横波 / 纵波
学完你会：给一个波，判断它是横波还是纵波，并说出理由。By the end: given any wave, you can say whether it is transverse or longitudinal, and why.
说人话：关键只看一件事——介质里的粒子往哪个方向动。横波：粒子上下动，波往前走，两个方向垂直。纵波：粒子前后动，波也往前走，两个方向平行。声音就是空气分子前后挤来挤去，所以是纵波。 In plain words: only one thing matters — which way the particles of the medium move. Transverse: particles move up and down while the wave moves forwards, so the two are perpendicular. Longitudinal: particles move back and forth along the same line the wave travels, so the two are parallel. Sound is air molecules shoving back and forth, so it is longitudinal.
记住这几点：Remember these:
  • 横波：振动 ⟂ 传播。光、绳波、水波。Transverse: vibration ⟂ travel. Light, rope waves, water waves.
  • 纵波：振动 ∥ 传播。声波、弹簧推拉波。Longitudinal: vibration ∥ travel. Sound, waves on a pushed spring.
  • 纵波里有密部和疏部，密部之间的距离就是一个 λ。A longitudinal wave has compressions and rarefactions; the distance between compressions is one λ.
  • 波传能量不传物质——这句话解释题反复考。Waves transfer energy, not matter — this sentence comes up again and again in "explain" questions.
🗣 考场英文怎么说（横波/纵波的定义题）：🗣 How to say it in the exam (defining transverse / longitudinal): In a transverse wave, the particles vibrate at right angles to the direction of energy transfer. In a longitudinal wave, the particles vibrate parallel to the direction of energy transfer. A wave transfers energy without transferring matter.

例题Example

分子在自己的平衡位置附近来回振动（前后），不是一路向前跑。The molecule oscillates about its equilibrium position (back and forth); it does not travel forwards. 所以不会被吹到耳朵里。传过去的是能量和振动状态，空气本身留在原地。So no, it is not blown into the ear. What travels is energy and the pattern of vibration; the air itself stays put.

3 偏振：只有横波能做到Polarisation: only transverse waves can do it Polarisation偏振
学完你会：解释为什么偏振能证明光是横波，以及为什么声音不能偏振。By the end: you can explain why polarisation proves light is transverse, and why sound cannot be polarised.
说人话：拿一根绳子穿过栅栏的竖缝。你上下抖绳子，波能过去；你左右抖，波被栅栏挡住。偏振片就是这道栅栏。声音是纵波，它的振动方向和传播方向同一条线，没有"横着的方向"可以被筛掉，所以永远偏振不了。 In plain words: thread a rope through the vertical slots of a fence. Shake it up and down and the wave passes; shake it side to side and the fence blocks it. A polariser is that fence. Sound is longitudinal — its vibration lies along the same line as its travel, so there is no sideways direction to filter out, and it can never be polarised.
记住这几点：Remember these:
  • 偏振只发生在横波上——这是横波的判据。Only transverse waves can be polarised — that is the test.
  • 偏振片转 90°，透过的光强降到 0。Turn the polariser 90° and the transmitted intensity falls to zero.
  • 应用：偏光太阳镜、液晶屏、3D 电影眼镜。Uses: polarised sunglasses, LCD screens, 3D cinema glasses.
🗣 考场英文怎么说：🗣 How to say it in the exam: Polarisation can only occur for transverse waves. Sound is a longitudinal wave, so it cannot be polarised. When the polariser is rotated through 90°, the intensity falls to zero.

4 叠加原理The principle of superposition Superposition叠加
学完你会：判断两列波叠加后是变强还是变弱，并说出"相加"这个核心动作。By the end: you can say whether two waves will reinforce or cancel, and name the key word — "add".
说人话：两个波在同一根绳子上相遇，绳子上每一点的位移就是两个波各自造成的位移加起来。峰加峰 = 更大的峰（相长）；峰加谷 = 互相抵消（相消）。最妙的是：它们穿过彼此之后，形状完全不变，就像从没遇见过一样。 In plain words: when two waves meet on the same rope, the displacement at every point is the sum of the displacements each wave would have produced alone. Peak plus peak = a bigger peak (constructive); peak plus trough = cancellation (destructive). The subtle part: after they pass through each other, both are completely unchanged, as if they had never met.
记住这几点：Remember these:
  • 叠加原理：合位移 = 各分位移的矢量和。Principle of superposition: the resultant displacement is the vector sum of the individual displacements.
  • 相长（同相 / 路程差 = nλ）：振幅变大。Constructive (in phase / path difference = nλ): larger amplitude.
  • 相消（反相 / 路程差 = (n+½)λ）：振幅变小甚至为 0。Destructive (out of phase / path difference = (n+½)λ): smaller amplitude, possibly zero.
  • 相遇后波形不受影响，各自继续传播。After meeting, the waves are unaffected and carry on.
🗣 考场英文怎么说：🗣 How to say it in the exam: When two waves meet, the resultant displacement is the sum of the individual displacements. Constructive interference occurs when the waves are in phase, giving a larger amplitude. Destructive interference occurs when the waves are in antiphase, giving a smaller (or zero) amplitude.

例题Example

相长：3 + 2 = 5 cmConstructive: 3 + 2 = 5 cm 相消：3 − 2 = 1 cm（不是 0！只有两列振幅相等才抵消到 0）Destructive: 3 − 2 = 1 cm (not zero! cancellation to zero only happens when the amplitudes are equal)

5 干涉与双缝实验Interference and the double-slit Two-source interference双缝干涉
学完你会：用 λ = ax/D 算波长，并判断某条是亮纹还是暗纹。By the end: you can use λ = ax/D to find the wavelength, and say whether a fringe is bright or dark.
说人话：两个缝出来的波是同步的（相干）。走到屏上某点，一个可能走了 1000 个波，另一个走了 1000.5 个波——差半个波长，一个的峰正好对上另一个的谷，就暗了。差整数个波长，峰对峰，就亮了。所以屏上出现一圈一圈明暗相间的条纹。 In plain words: the waves leaving the two slits are in step (coherent). 6 衍射：绕过角落Diffraction: bending round corners Diffraction衍射
学完你会：说出衍射最明显的条件，并解释生活中的现象。By the end: you can state when diffraction is most noticeable, and explain everyday examples.
说人话：波遇到障碍物或狭缝时会"拐弯"，这叫衍射。拐得多不多，取决于缝的大小和波长是不是差不多。声波波长几十厘米到几米，跟门缝、墙角尺寸相仿，所以能绕过去；光的波长只有几百纳米，比门缝小一百万倍，几乎只走直线——所以能隔墙听见人说话，却看不见人。 In plain words: waves bend round obstacles or through gaps — that is diffraction. How much they bend depends on whether the gap size and the wavelength are comparable. Sound wavelengths range from tens of centimetres to metres, similar to a doorway or a corner, so sound bends round them; light's wavelength is a few hundred nanometres, a million times smaller than a doorway, so it travels essentially straight — which is why you can hear people through a wall but not see them.
记住这几点：Remember these:
  • 衍射最明显的条件：缝宽（或障碍物尺寸）≈ 波长。Diffraction is most noticeable when the gap (or obstacle) is about the same size as the wavelength.
  • 缝越窄、波长越长 → 散得越开。Narrower gap or longer wavelength → wider spread.
  • 衍射不改变波速、频率和波长，只改变传播方向。Diffraction does not change speed, frequency or wavelength — only the direction of travel.
🗣 考场英文怎么说：🗣 How to say it in the exam: Diffraction is the spreading of a wave as it passes through a gap or around an edge. Diffraction is most significant when the wavelength is comparable to the size of the gap. Diffraction does not change the speed, frequency or wavelength of the wave.

3 电阻与欧姆定律Resistance and Ohm's law Ohm's law欧姆定律
学完你会：用 V = IR 三向求解，并判断元件是否遵守欧姆定律。By the end: you can use V = IR in all three directions, and say whether a component is ohmic.
说人话：电阻就是"这个元件有多挡路"。电压是推它的力，电流是实际流过去的量——同样的推力，挡得越狠，流过去的越少。欧姆定律说的是：对金属这类"听话"的元件，推力（V）加倍，流量（I）也加倍，R = V/I 始终不变。要注意因果：R 是元件自己的属性，不是由 V 或 I 决定的，只是恰好可以用 V/I 算出来。 In plain words: resistance is "how much this component gets in the way". The p.d. is the push, the current is what actually flows — with the same push, more obstruction means less flow. Ohm's law says that for well-behaved components such as metals, doubling the push (V) doubles the flow (I), so R = V/I stays constant. Mind the logic though: R is a property of the component; it is not caused by V or I, it just happens to equal V/I.
记住这几点：Remember these:
  • V = I R，三个量知道两个求第三个。— know any two, find the third.
  • 欧姆定律：恒温下，金属导体的 V ∝ I。Ohm's law: at constant temperature, V ∝ I for a metallic conductor.
  • R 不随 V 变——R = V/I 只是算法，不是因果关系。R does not change with V — R = V/I is how you measure it, not what causes it.
  • P = V I = I²R = V²/R，功率三式等价。— three equivalent ways to write power.
🗣 考场英文怎么说：🗣 How to say it in the exam: Ohm's law states that the current in a conductor is proportional to the potential difference across it, provided the temperature is constant. The resistance is the ratio of p.d. to current; it is a property of the component. A component is ohmic if its V–I graph is a straight line through the origin.

5 电阻率 ρ = RA / LResistivity ρ = RA / L Resistivity电阻率

6 串并联电路Series and parallel circuits Series & parallel串联与并联
学完你会：算串并联总电阻，并判断电流、电压在两种接法下怎么分。By the end: you can find the combined resistance, and say how current and p.d. split in each arrangement.
说人话：串联就是一条独木桥，所有人排一队过——所以电流处处相同，但每人消耗的力气（电压）加起来等于总电压。并联是开了两条岔路，每条路两端的高度差一样——所以电压相同，人分成两股（电流分）。并联的总电阻反而变小，因为多开了一条路，整体更好走了。 In plain words: series is a single plank bridge — everyone queues up to cross, so the current is the same everywhere, but the effort (p.d.) each person uses adds up to the total. Parallel is two diverging paths with the same height difference across each, so the p.d. is the same and the crowd splits (the current divides). The combined resistance in parallel is smaller, because an extra path makes the journey easier overall.
记住这几点：Remember these:
  • 串联：I 相同；V 分；R = R₁ + R₂ + …Series: same I; V splits; R = R₁ + R₂ + …
  • 并联：V 相同；I 分；1/R = 1/R₁ + 1/R₂Parallel: same V; I splits; 1/R = 1/R₁ + 1/R₂
  • 两个并联快捷式：R = R₁R₂/(R₁+R₂)。Shortcut for two: R = R₁R₂/(R₁+R₂).
  • 并联总电阻永远小于最小的那一个——用它自检。The parallel total is always less than the smallest branch — use that as a check.

学完你会：用 E = V + Ir 算内阻，用分压公式算输出电压。By the end: you can use E = V + Ir to find internal resistance, and the divider formula for an output p.d.
说人话：电池内部也有电阻，叫内阻 r。电流 I 一流动，就在电池内部先"扣掉" I r 这么多电压，所以外面测到的端电压 V = E − I r。这就是为什么旧电池空载测着还有 1.5 V，一接上电机就掉到 1.1 V——旧电池内阻大，一拉电流就垮。分压器则是用两个电阻串联，从总电压里"切"出需要的那一小份。 In plain words: a cell has resistance of its own, the internal resistance r. 2 核符号与同位素：A、Z、NNuclear notation and isotopes: A, Z, N Notation核符号
学完你会：看到任何一个核符号，立刻说出质子、中子、电子各有多少。By the end: given any nuclide symbol, you can state the number of protons, neutrons and electrons at once.

3 三种辐射：α、β、γThe three types of radiation: α, β, γ α, β, γ三种辐射
学完你会：背下三种辐射的对照表，并会用"纸 / 铝 / 铅"设计鉴别方案。By the end: you know the comparison table by heart and can identify a source using paper, aluminium and lead.

说人话：半衰期就是"减半所需的时间"。它非常顽固——加热、加压、磨成粉、溶解，都没法让它变快或变慢，因为这是核内部的事，跟核外电子（化学反应那一层）完全无关。做题有两条路：快速法——数经历了几个半衰期，就连续除以几个 2；精确法——用 N = N₀e⁻λt，其中 λ = ln2 / t½。考试里若给的时间不是半衰期的整数倍，就必须用精确法。 In plain words: the half-life is "the time it takes to halve". It is remarkably stubborn — heating, pressurising, grinding or dissolving makes no difference, because decay is a nuclear process, nothing to do with the outer electrons where chemistry happens. There are two ways to answer: the quick way — count how many half-lives have passed and keep dividing by 2; the exact way — N = N₀e⁻λt, with λ = ln 2 / t½. If the time in the question is not a whole number of half-lives, you must use the exact method.
记住这几点：Remember these:
  • 定义：半衰期 = 样品中未衰变核数（或活度）减半所需的时间。Definition: the half-life is the time for the number of undecayed nuclei (or the activity) to halve.
  • N = N₀ e⁻λt，活度、计数率都遵循同一条规律。, and the activity and count rate follow exactly the same law.
  • λ = ln 2 / t½，λ 叫衰变常数，单位是 s⁻¹。, where λ is the decay constant, measured in s⁻¹.
  • 衰变是随机的：对单个原子核无法预测，对大量原子核才能统计。Decay is random: unpredictable for one nucleus, statistical for many.
🗣 考场英文怎么说How to say it in the exam Decay is a random process: we cannot predict when a particular nucleus will decay.
The half-life is the time taken for the number of undecayed nuclei to halve.
The half-life is unaffected by temperature, pressure or chemical state.

Because c² is enormous (9 × 10¹⁶), a tiny mass buys a huge amount of energy — that is the basis of both nuclear power stations and nuclear weapons. 7 结合能、裂变与聚变Binding energy, fission and fusion Binding energy结合能
学完你会：解释为什么裂变和聚变都能放能，并会算每核子结合能。By the end: you can explain why both fission and fusion release energy, and calculate binding energy per nucleon.
说人话：结合能就是"把原子核拆成一堆散零件需要的能量"，反过来说也是"这些零件凑成核时放出的能量"。结合得越紧，能量越低，也就越稳。把"每个核子平均摊到多少结合能"画成图，就得到那条著名的曲线：铁-56 附近最高（约 8.8 MeV），两边都低。于是自然界只有两条"下坡路"：轻核聚在一起（聚变，太阳在干的事），重核裂成两半（裂变，核电站在干的事）——两者都是往山顶走，走上去多出来的能量就放出来。 In plain words: the binding energy is the energy needed to pull a nucleus apart into separate nucleons — or, looking the other way, the energy released when those nucleons come together. The more tightly bound, the lower the energy, and the more stable the nucleus. Plot the binding energy per nucleon and you get the famous curve: a peak near iron-56 (about 8.8 MeV), lower at both ends. So nature has only two downhill routes: light nuclei joining up (fusion, what the Sun does) and heavy nuclei splitting in two (fission, what a power station does). Both move towards the peak, and the extra binding energy is released on the way.
记住这几点：Remember these:
  • 结合能 = 把核拆散所需能量 = Δm c²。Binding energy = energy to separate the nucleons = Δm c².
  • 每核子结合能 = 结合能 ÷ A，越大越稳定；铁-56 最大。Binding energy per nucleon = binding energy ÷ A; bigger means more stable; iron-56 is the maximum.
  • 裂变：重核（如 ²³⁵U 吸收一个中子）裂成两个中等核 + 几个中子。Fission: a heavy nucleus (e.g. U-235 absorbing a neutron) splits into two medium nuclei plus neutrons.
  • 聚变：轻核（如氘 + 氚）合成较重的核，需要极高温度克服斥力。Fusion: light nuclei (e.g. deuterium + tritium) join together, needing extreme temperature to overcome repulsion.
特别提醒：Watch out: 别把"结合能大"和"结合能/核子大"混为一谈。铀的总结合能比铁大得多，但摊到 235 个核子上就不如铁紧，所以铀不稳定、能裂变。比的是人均，不是总量。 Do not confuse total binding energy with binding energy per nucleon. Uranium has a far larger total binding energy than iron, but shared over 235 nucleons it is less tightly bound, which is why uranium can release energy by fission. Compare the per-nucleon figure, not the total.

3 误差：随机 vs 系统Errors: random vs systematic Errors误差
学完你会：判断一个误差是随机还是系统，并说出对应的补救办法。By the end: you can classify an error as random or systematic and say how to deal with it.
说人话：把测量想象成打靶。随机误差像手抖：这一枪偏左、下一枪偏右，但多打几枪、取平均位置，就越来越接近靶心——所以多次测量取平均是它的解药。系统误差像瞄歪了的准星：每一枪都很整齐，但整体偏到一边去了，这时候再怎么平均都没用，必须修仪器、校零点、改方法。考试只要问"如何减小误差"，先分清是哪一类再答。 In plain words: think of measuring as shooting at a target. Random error is a shaky hand: one shot left, the next right, but the average of many shots closes in on the bullseye — so repeat and average is the cure. Systematic error is a misaligned sight: every shot lands neatly, but all of them off to one side. Averaging will not help at all; you must fix the instrument, correct the zero, or change the method. When a question asks how to reduce error, classify it first, then answer.
记住这几点：Remember these:
  • 随机误差 → 结果上下波动 → 解药是重复测量取平均、画最佳拟合线。Random error → readings scatter either side → cure: repeat and average, draw a best-fit line.
  • 系统误差 → 结果一致偏向一边 → 解药是校准、扣零误差、改实验设计。Systematic error → results all shift the same way → cure: calibrate, correct the zero, redesign.
  • 准确度 accuracy = 离真值多近；精密度 precision = 数据多集中。Accuracy = closeness to the true value; precision = how tightly the readings agree.
  • "误差 error" ≠ "错误 mistake"：读错数、算错数是错误，不在误差讨论范围内。An error is not a mistake: misreading or miscalculating is a blunder, not an error in this sense.
🗣 考场英文怎么说How to say it in the exam A random error causes readings to be spread about the true value; it can be reduced by repeating and averaging.
A systematic error causes all readings to be shifted by the same amount; repeating does not reduce it — the instrument must be recalibrated or the zero error corrected.

4 不确定度怎么算Working out uncertainty Uncertainty不确定度
学完你会：给任何计算结果配上不确定度，并找出"误差主要来自哪一项"。By the end: you can attach an uncertainty to any calculated result and spot which measurement dominates the error.
说人话：不确定度就是"这个答案大概能差多少"。两个量相加相减时，误差按绝对量叠加（两个 ±1 mm 相减变成 ±2 mm）；相乘相除时，误差按百分比叠加（2% 乘上 3% 变成 5%）。它最有价值的地方不是最后那个 ±，而是能告诉你该先去改进哪一项——百分比最大的那个量就是瓶颈，把别的量做到 0.1% 也白搭。 In plain words: the uncertainty says roughly how far your answer might be off. When you add or subtract, absolute uncertainties add (±1 mm minus ±1 mm gives ±2 mm); when you multiply or divide, percentage uncertainties add (2% times 3% gives 5%). The real value is not the final ± but what it tells you: the term with the largest percentage is the bottleneck, and improving any other measurement to 0.1% achieves nothing.
记住这几点：Remember these:
  • 单次读数：Δx = 仪器精度（如 ±1 mm、±0.01 mm）。Single reading: Δx = the instrument's precision (±1 mm, ±0.01 mm, …).
  • 多次读数：Δx = (最大值 − 最小值) / 2。Repeated readings: Δx = (max − min) / 2.
  • 加减 → 绝对不确定度相加；乘除 → 百分比不确定度相加；次方 → 百分比 × 指数。Add/subtract → add absolute; multiply/divide → add percentages; powers → percentage × index.
  • 结果写成 x = 值 ± Δ值 单位，两者的小数位数要对齐（如 1.23 ± 0.04 m）。Write the result as x = value ± Δvalue unit, with matching decimal places (e.g. 1.23 ± 0.04 m).
特别提醒：Watch out: "取平均"能减小随机误差带来的不确定度，但仪器精度那一项不会因为多测几次而消失。用"一半极差"算出的 Δx 如果小于仪器精度，就老老实实写仪器精度。 Averaging reduces the uncertainty from random scatter, but it cannot remove the instrument's precision. If half the range comes out smaller than the instrument precision, quote the instrument precision.

5 作图的规矩Rules for drawing graphs Graphs作图
学完你会：按评分标准画出一张能拿满分的图，并正确求出梯度。By the end: you can produce a full-marks graph and find its gradient correctly.
说人话：图是给阅卷老师看的第一印象，规矩比内容还重要。轴上必须写"物理量 / 单位"；刻度要选得让点铺满大半张纸（不要用 1、2、3 这种浪费纸的刻度，也不必非从 0 开始）；点画成细细的叉或小点加圈，别涂成大圆团；然后画一条从点中间穿过的直线，而不是把点连成折线。求梯度时，在拟合线上取相距尽量远的两点（至少占半张纸），而不是拿原始数据点——这是最常被扣分的一条。 In plain words: the graph is the examiner's first impression, so the rules matter as much as the physics. Label each axis "quantity / unit"; choose scales so the points fill most of the page (no need to start at zero); plot small neat crosses, not fat blobs; then draw one straight line through the middle of the points rather than joining them up. When finding the gradient, pick two points far apart on your line (at least half the page), not the raw data points — this is the most commonly penalised step.
记住这几点：Remember these:
  • 横轴 = 自变量（你主动改变的量），纵轴 = 因变量（随之改变的量）。Horizontal = the independent variable (what you change), vertical = the dependent variable (what responds).
  • 刻度用 1、2、5 的整倍数（1、2、5、10、20…），别用 3、7 这种难读的。Use scales in steps of 1, 2 or 5 (×10ⁿ) — never 3 or 7.
  • 有误差棒就画上，它表示该点的不确定度，拟合线应穿过大多数误差棒。Draw error bars when you have them; the line should pass through most bars.
  • 明显的异常点要圈出来并说明，但不要擅自删掉重画。Circle any clear anomalous point and comment on it — do not quietly delete it.

6 用梯度与截距求物理量Finding a quantity from gradient or intercept y = mx + c直线化
学完你会：把任意直线关系翻译成"梯度等于什么"，并反解出物理量。By the end: you can translate any straight-line relationship into "the gradient equals …" and solve for the quantity.
说人话：这一站是整个实验部分的"万能钥匙"。原理只有一句：只要把公式摆成 y = mx + c，m 和 c 就自动变成了你想要的物理量的某种组合。比如 V = E − Ir：把 V 当 y、I 当 x，那么截距就是电动势 E，梯度的绝对值就是内阻 r（负号表示电流越大、端电压越低）。考试最舒服的地方在于：你不用先知道答案，画完直线答案就写在图上了。 In plain words: this stop is the skeleton key to the whole practical paper. The idea is one line: once the formula reads y = mx + c, m and c automatically become combinations of the quantity you want. Take V = E − Ir: with V as y and I as x, the intercept is the e.m.f. E and the magnitude of the gradient is the internal resistance r (the minus sign says the terminal p.d. falls as the current rises). The pleasant part is that you do not need to know the answer in advance — draw the line and it tells you.
记住这几点：Remember these:
  • 先变形再画图：公式里有根号、平方、分式的，先想办法拉直。Rearrange before plotting: if the formula has roots, squares or fractions, linearise it first.
  • 截距 c 必须看坐标轴是否从 0 开始，横轴不从 0 起时截距要小心。When reading the intercept, check whether the axis starts at zero.
  • 梯度 = Δy / Δx，用线上相距远的两点，并带上单位。Gradient = Δy / Δx, from two widely separated points on the line, with its unit.
  • 过原点说明成正比；不过原点往往代表有个初始偏置（如零误差）。Through the origin means proportional; a non-zero intercept often signals a systematic offset such as a zero error.

7 实验设计与改进（6 分题套路）Designing and improving an experiment Planning设计
学完你会：独立写出一份完整的实验方案，并针对具体问题给出有效的改进建议。By the end: you can write a complete plan on your own and suggest improvements that address specific errors.
说人话：实验设计题不是考你"有没有灵感"，而是考你有没有清单。先把变量分三类：自变量（只有它变）、因变量（你去测它）、控制变量（其他统统按住不动）。然后照清单往下写：装置怎么搭、每个量用什么仪器测、取至少 6 组数据、重复取平均、画图标误差棒、最后说安全。至于"改进"，必须指着具体的误差说——"数 20 个周期再除"针对反应时间，"量到球心"针对系统误差，而"我会更小心"等于没说。 In plain words: the planning question does not test inspiration, it tests whether you have a checklist. Start by sorting the variables: the independent variable (the only thing you change), the dependent variable (what you measure), and the control variables (everything else, held firmly still). Then work down the list: how the apparatus is set up, which instrument measures each quantity, at least six data sets, repeats to average, a graph with error bars, and finally safety. As for improvements, name the specific error: "time 20 oscillations and divide" tackles reaction time, "measure to the centre of the bob" tackles a systematic error, and "be more careful" says nothing at all.
记住这几点：Remember these:
  • 变量三分类：自变量 / 因变量 / 控制变量，写方案时逐一交代。Sort the variables into independent / dependent / controlled and state each one.
  • 数据至少 6 组，覆盖范围尽量大（够画一条可信的直线）。Take at least six sets of data over as wide a range as possible.
  • 能画图就画图：图比表格更能说明规律，也更容易拿梯度分。Plot a graph wherever you can: it shows the pattern better than a table and earns gradient marks.
  • 安全要具体：护目镜、配重防倒、夹稳、别让重物砸脚、热源隔离。Make safety specific: goggles, counterweights, firm clamping, keeping feet clear, shielding heat sources.

★ 没有实验室？在家也能做的 10 个实验No lab? 10 experiments you can do at home Home practicals家庭实验

器材都是家里能找到的。每个实验都按 A-Level 实验题的套路来做：改一个自变量 → 测因变量 → 重复取平均 → 画图 → 求斜率。做完顺手把数据、表格、图、结论写下来，就是一份完整的实验报告练习。All apparatus is stuff you have at home. Follow the A-level practical pattern each time: change one independent variable → measure the dependent one → repeat and average → plot a graph → find the gradient. Write up the data, table, graph and conclusion and you have a full report-writing exercise.

  1. 单摆测 g：绳子 + 小重物，改摆长 L，测 20 个周期求 T，画 T² 对 L 的图，斜率 = 4π²/g。（对应单元 1、6）Measuring g with a pendulum: string plus a small mass, vary length L, time 20 oscillations for T, plot T² against L — gradient = 4π²/g. (Units 1, 6)
  2. 弹簧的胡克定律：弹簧 + 尺子 + 一堆硬币，加砝码测伸长，画 F 对 x 的图，斜率 = k。（对应单元 2）Hooke's law with a spring: spring, ruler and coins; add load and measure extension; plot F against x — gradient = k. (Unit 2)
  3. 橡皮筋的滞回：加载和卸载各测一次，看看两条曲线不重合（弹性滞后）。Rubber-band hysteresis: measure loading and unloading; the two curves do not coincide (elastic hysteresis).
  4. 纸的承重：把纸折成不同形状（平板/瓦楞/圆筒），测能承受多少本书——材料与结构。（对应单元 2）How much load can paper take? Fold it flat, corrugated, or into cylinders and load with books — materials and structure. (Unit 2)
  5. 水面波：洗脸盆装水，用尺子敲水面制造波，观察反射、衍射（障碍物）、干涉（两个手指同时敲）。（对应单元 3）Waves on water: a basin of water; tap the surface with a ruler to see reflection, diffraction around an obstacle, and interference from two fingers. (Unit 3)
  6. 玻璃杯与筷子：看折射与「折断」的筷子；用手电筒看全反射。（对应单元 3 之后的几何光学）Glass and chopsticks: refraction and the "broken" chopstick; total internal reflection with a torch. (Geometrical optics after Unit 3)
  7. 电阻丝的长度：电池 + 灯泡 + 铅笔芯或电阻丝，改长度看亮度变化——电阻与长度成正比。（对应单元 4）Resistance and length: a cell, a bulb and a pencil lead or resistance wire — change the length and watch the brightness; R ∝ length. (Unit 4)
  8. 水果电池：柠檬 + 铜片 + 锌片，测电压，几个串起来看电压怎么变（内阻与串联）。（对应单元 4）Fruit battery: lemon with copper and zinc electrodes; measure the e.m.f. and connect several in series — internal resistance and series cells. (Unit 4)
  9. 手机测重力加速度：用手机里的加速度传感器 App（如 phyphox）做自由落体或电梯升降的测量。Phone as a sensor: use a sensor app such as phyphox to record free fall or a lift accelerating.
  10. 冷却曲线：一杯热水每分钟记一次温度，画 T 对 t 的图，看降温速率怎么随温差变化。Cooling curve: a cup of hot water, temperature every minute; plot T against t and watch how the rate of cooling depends on the temperature difference.

进阶：phyphox（德国亚琛工大做的免费手机物理实验 App）能把你手机里的加速度、陀螺仪、麦克风变成测量仪器，做声速、单摆、磁场的实验，官网 phyphox.org。Next level: phyphox, a free app from RWTH Aachen that turns your phone's accelerometer, gyroscope and microphone into measuring instruments for sound speed, pendulum and magnetic-field experiments — see phyphox.org.
