五、考试提示5 · Exam tips

  • P1 抢分靠熟：40 题 75 分钟，每题不到 2 分钟，概念题和简单计算要秒出，不会的先跳过回头再做。P1 rewards speed: 40 questions in 75 minutes means under 2 minutes each — knock out the concept and simple calculation questions fast, skip and return to the hard ones.
  • P2/P4 靠过程：大题按「摩尔 → 方程式 → 计算 → 单位」写清步骤，中间步骤有分；单位（mol、mol dm−3、kJ mol−1）一定写上。P2/P4 reward working: write "moles → equation → calculation → unit" step by step; the working earns marks. Always give units (mol, mol dm−3, kJ mol−1).
  • 方程式必须配平：写状态符号 (s)/(l)/(g)/(aq)，配平 + 状态符号是白送分，别丢。Balance every equation: add state symbols (s)/(l)/(g)/(aq); balancing + state symbols are free marks.
  • P3/P5 靠实验思维：会设计实验、读刻度、算误差、判断结果是否可靠并提改进。P3/P5 reward experimental thinking: designing experiments, reading apparatus, calculating errors, judging reliability and suggesting improvements.

1[6]

写出氯原子 Cl（Z = 17）的完整电子排布（用 s、p 轨道表示）。Write the full electron configuration of a chlorine atom Cl (Z = 17) using s and p orbitals.

点开看答案Show answer
1s2 2s2 2p6 3s2 3p5

共 2 + 2 + 6 + 2 + 5 = 17 个电子，与原子序数一致。记住「每层按 1s → 2s → 2p → 3s → 3p」顺序填。2 + 2 + 6 + 2 + 5 = 17 electrons, matching the atomic number. Fill in the order 1s → 2s → 2p → 3s → 3p.

2[6]

(a) 计算 8.0 g NaOH（Mr = 40）的物质的量（mol）。(a) Calculate the amount (mol) of 8.0 g NaOH (Mr = 40).

(b) 把它配成 500 cm³ 溶液，求浓度（mol dm−3）。(b) It is dissolved to make 500 cm³ of solution. Find the concentration (mol dm−3).

点开看答案Show answer
(a) n = m / Mr = 8.0 / 40 = 0.20 mol (b) c = n / V = 0.20 / 0.500 = 0.40 mol dm−3

注意体积要换成 dm³：500 cm³ = 0.500 dm³。套公式 n = m/Mr，再 c = n/V。Convert the volume to dm³ first: 500 cm³ = 0.500 dm³. Use n = m/Mr, then c = n/V.

3[6]

用 VSEPR 理论说出 NH3 的分子形状和键角，并解释原因。Using VSEPR theory, state the shape and bond angle of NH3, and explain why.

点开看答案Show answer

形状：三角锥形（trigonal pyramidal）；键角 107°。原因：N 周围有 3 对成键电子 + 1 对孤对电子；孤对电子排斥力更强，把三对成键电子压近，所以键角从正四面体的 109.5° 压缩到 107°。Shape: trigonal pyramidal; bond angle 107°. Reason: N has 3 bonding pairs + 1 lone pair; the lone pair repels more strongly, pushing the three bonding pairs closer, so the angle is reduced from 109.5° to 107°.

4[6]

对平衡 aA + bB ⇌ cC + dD，写出平衡常数 Kc 的表达式。Write the equilibrium constant Kc expression for the equilibrium aA + bB ⇌ cC + dD.

点开看答案Show answer
Kc = [C]c[D]d / [A]a[B]b

规律：产物浓度在分子、反应物浓度在分母，各自的系数变成幂。方括号 [ ] 表示平衡时的浓度（mol dm−3）。Rule: products on top, reactants on the bottom, each raised to the power of its coefficient. Square brackets [ ] mean the concentration at equilibrium (mol dm−3).

5[6]

计算 0.0100 mol dm−3 HCl 溶液的 pH。Calculate the pH of a 0.0100 mol dm−3 solution of HCl.

点开看答案Show answer
[H+] = 0.0100 mol dm−3 pH = −log[H+] = −log(0.0100) = 2.00

HCl 是强酸，完全电离，所以 [H+] = 酸的浓度。pH = −log[H+]，保留两位小数。HCl is a strong acid and dissociates fully, so [H+] equals the acid concentration. pH = −log[H+], to two decimal places.

6[6]

(a) 给化合物 CH3CH2COOH 用 IUPAC 规则命名。(a) Name the compound CH3CH2COOH using IUPAC rules.

(b) 写出它含有的官能团。(b) State the functional group it contains.

点开看答案Show answer

(a) propanoic acid（丙酸）：3 个碳的主链（prop-）+ 羧基（-oic acid）。(a) propanoic acid: a 3-carbon chain (prop-) with a carboxyl group (-oic acid).

(b) 官能团：羧基 –COOH（carboxyl group）。(b) Functional group: carboxyl group –COOH.

7[8]

写出并配平酸性条件下高锰酸根离子的还原半反应 MnO4− → Mn2+，并说出 Mn 的氧化数变化。Write and balance the half-equation for the reduction of manganate(VII) ions MnO4− → Mn2+ in acid, and state the change in oxidation number of Mn.

点开看答案Show answer
MnO4− + 8H+ + 5e− → Mn2+ + 4H2O

Mn 的氧化数从 +7（MnO4−）降到 +2（Mn2+），得到 5 个电子，是还原。配平顺序：先平 Mn，再用 H2O 平 O、用 H+ 平 H，最后加电子平电荷。Mn changes from +7 (in MnO4−) to +2 (in Mn2+), gaining 5 electrons — it is reduced. Balance Mn first, then O with H2O, H with H+, and finally electrons for charge.

8[6]

用键焓数据计算反应 H2 + Cl2 → 2HCl 的 ΔH。已知 E(H–H) = 436、E(Cl–Cl) = 243、E(H–Cl) = 431 kJ mol−1。Use bond enthalpies to calculate ΔH for H2 + Cl2 → 2HCl. Given E(H–H) = 436, E(Cl–Cl) = 243, E(H–Cl) = 431 kJ mol−1.

点开看答案Show answer
ΔH = 断键吸热 − 成键放热 = (436 + 243) − (2 × 431) = 679 − 862 = −183 kJ mol−1

口诀：断键吸热（+）、成键放热（−）。反应物断 1 个 H–H 和 1 个 Cl–Cl，产物形成 2 个 H–Cl（所以要乘 2）。结果为负，说明放热。Key idea: breaking bonds absorbs energy (+), making bonds releases energy (−). Reactants break one H–H and one Cl–Cl; products form two H–Cl (so ×2). A negative result means the reaction is exothermic.

A-Level 化学（9701）· 模拟卷 BA-Level Chemistry (9701) · Practice Paper B

时间 45 分钟 · 满分 50 分 · 每题的「点开看答案」在题下方Time 45 min · 50 marks · each answer opens below the question

1[6]

氯有两种同位素：35Cl 占 75%，37Cl 占 25%。计算氯的相对原子质量 Ar。Chlorine has two isotopes: 35Cl is 75% and 37Cl is 25%. Calculate the relative atomic mass Ar of chlorine.

点开看答案Show answer
Ar = (35 × 75 + 37 × 25) / 100 = (2625 + 925) / 100 = 3550 / 100 = 35.5

Ar 是各同位素质量按丰度加权平均的值，没有单位。这也解释了为什么周期表上氯的 Ar 是 35.5。Ar is the weighted average of isotope masses by abundance; it has no unit. This is why the periodic table lists chlorine as 35.5.

2[8]

用理想气体方程 pV = nRT，计算 0.5 mol 气体在 100 kPa、298 K 下的体积（R = 8.31 J K−1 mol−1）。分别以 m³ 和 dm³ 表示。Using the ideal gas equation pV = nRT, calculate the volume of 0.5 mol of gas at 100 kPa and 298 K (R = 8.31 J K−1 mol−1). Give the answer in m³ and dm³.

点开看答案Show answer
V = nRT / p = (0.5 × 8.31 × 298) / 100000 = 1238.19 / 100000 = 0.0124 m³ = 12.4 dm³

压强要换成 Pa：100 kPa = 100 000 Pa；R 用 8.31 时，V 的单位自动是 m³。1 m³ = 1000 dm³。Convert pressure to Pa: 100 kPa = 100 000 Pa. With R = 8.31, V comes out in m³. 1 m³ = 1000 dm³.

3[6]

解释催化剂如何加快化学反应速率。Explain how a catalyst increases the rate of a reaction.

点开看答案Show answer

催化剂提供一条活化能更低的替代反应路径。活化能降低后，能越过能垒的分子比例大大增加，有效碰撞变多，反应速率加快。催化剂本身在反应前后质量和化学性质不变。A catalyst provides an alternative pathway with a lower activation energy. With a lower energy barrier, a much larger fraction of molecules can react, so effective collisions increase and the rate rises. The catalyst itself is unchanged in mass and chemical nature at the end.

4[8]

描述第 2 族元素从 Mg 到 Ba：活泼性和碳酸盐热稳定性的变化趋势，并解释原因。Describe how, down Group 2 from Mg to Ba, the reactivity and the thermal stability of the carbonates change, and explain why.

点开看答案Show answer

活泼性：越往下越强（Ba 比 Mg 活泼）。原因：越往下原子半径越大，最外层电子离核越远、屏蔽越多，电离能降低，越容易失去 2 个电子形成离子。Reactivity increases down the group (Ba is more reactive than Mg). Reason: the atomic radius increases, so the outer electrons are farther from the nucleus with more shielding; the ionisation energy decreases and the atoms lose two electrons more easily.

碳酸盐热稳定性：越往下越稳定（BaCO3 最难分解）。原因：越往下阳离子电荷密度越低，对 CO32− 的极化能力越弱，碳酸根越难分解。Carbonate thermal stability increases down the group (BaCO3 is hardest to decompose). Reason: the cation charge density falls, so its polarising power on CO32− weakens, making the carbonate harder to break down.

5[8]

写出乙醇被酸化重铬酸钾氧化时的产物链（伯醇 → ? → ?），并说出观察到的颜色变化。Write the product chain when ethanol is oxidised by acidified potassium dichromate (primary alcohol → ? → ?), and state the colour change observed.

点开看答案Show answer
CH3CH2OH → CH3CHO → CH3COOH （伯醇）→（醛）→（羧酸）

伯醇先被氧化成醛（ethanal 乙醛），继续氧化成羧酸（ethanoic acid 乙酸）。颜色从橙色（Cr2O72−）变成绿色（Cr3+）。A primary alcohol is first oxidised to an aldehyde (ethanal), then further to a carboxylic acid (ethanoic acid). The colour changes from orange (Cr2O72−) to green (Cr3+).

6[6]

在红外光谱（IR）中，C=O 的特征吸收大致在哪个波数范围？In infrared (IR) spectroscopy, roughly what wavenumber range is characteristic of the C=O stretch?

点开看答案Show answer

1680–1750 cm−1，是羰基（C=O）的强吸收峰，常用于识别醛、酮、羧酸、酯等含羰基的化合物。1680–1750 cm−1 — a strong absorption from the carbonyl (C=O) bond, used to identify aldehydes, ketones, carboxylic acids, esters and other carbonyl compounds.

7[4]

用勒夏特列原理说明：对一个放热反应，升高温度平衡会向哪个方向移动？Using Le Chatelier's principle, state which way the equilibrium of an exothermic reaction shifts when the temperature is raised.

点开看答案Show answer

升温使平衡向吸热方向移动。对放热反应（正反应放热），吸热方向就是逆反应方向，所以平衡逆向移动，产物的量减少。Raising the temperature shifts the equilibrium in the endothermic direction. For an exothermic reaction (forward reaction gives out heat), that direction is the reverse reaction, so the equilibrium shifts left and the amount of product decreases.

8[4]

电解熔融 NaCl 时，写出阴极和阳极各得到什么产物。During the electrolysis of molten NaCl, state the product at the cathode and at the anode.

点开看答案Show answer

阴极（−）：钠 Na——Na+ 得电子被还原：Na+ + e− → Na。Cathode (−): sodium, Na — Na+ gains electrons and is reduced: Na+ + e− → Na.

阳极（+）：氯气 Cl2——Cl− 失电子被氧化：2Cl− → Cl2 + 2e−。Anode (+): chlorine gas, Cl2 — Cl− loses electrons and is oxidised: 2Cl− → Cl2 + 2e−.
