四、考试提示4 · Exam tips

  • Paper 1 靠熟练：40 题 75 分钟，平均每题不到 2 分钟。单位换算、量纲、公式要一看就会，不能现场推导。Paper 1 rewards fluency: 40 questions in 75 minutes — under 2 minutes each. Unit conversions, dimensions and formulas must be instant.
  • Paper 2 / 4 靠步骤：结构化题按「写公式 → 代值 → 答案 + 单位」走，漏单位会扣分。Papers 2 & 4 reward method: write the formula, substitute, then give the answer with its unit — missing units lose marks.
  • Paper 3 / 5 靠实验：会读仪器刻度、算不确定度、写误差来源。AO3 的分数几乎都在这两张卷。Papers 3 & 5 reward practical skills: read instruments, estimate uncertainty, state sources of error — nearly all AO3 marks live here.
  • g 用 9.8：除非题目另给，重力加速度取 g = 9.8 m s-2，并保留合适的有效数字。Use g = 9.8: unless stated otherwise, take g = 9.8 m s-2 and keep sensible significant figures.

1[6]

(a) 把速度 36 km/h 换算成 m/s。(a) Convert the speed 36 km/h into m/s.

(b) 用基本单位（kg、m、s）说明 1 N 等于什么。(b) Use base units (kg, m, s) to state what 1 N is equal to.

点开看答案Show answer

(a) 1 km = 1000 m，1 h = 3600 s，代入换算：(a) 1 km = 1000 m and 1 h = 3600 s, so:

36 km/h = 36 × (1000 m / 3600 s) = 10 m s-1

(b) 由牛顿第二定律 F = ma，把单位代入：(b) From Newton's second law F = ma, substituting units:

1 N = 1 kg × 1 m s-2 = 1 kg m s-2
2[6]

物体从静止（u = 0）出发，以加速度 a = 2 m s-2 运动 t = 5 s。An object starts from rest (u = 0) and accelerates at a = 2 m s-2 for t = 5 s.

(a) 求末速度 v。(a) Find the final velocity v.

(b) 求这段时间内的位移 s。(b) Find the displacement s in this time.

点开看答案Show answer

(a) 匀加速第一式 v = u + at；u = 0：(a) First SUVAT equation v = u + at, with u = 0:

v = u + at = 0 + 2 × 5 = 10 m s-1

(b) 位移 s = ut + ½at²；u = 0：(b) Displacement s = ut + ½at², with u = 0:

s = ut + ½at² = 0 + ½ × 2 × 5² = 25 m
3[8]

(a) 质量 5 kg 的物体受到 20 N 的合力，求它的加速度。(a) A 5 kg object is acted on by a resultant force of 20 N. Find its acceleration.

(b) 陈述牛顿第三定律，并举一个例子。(b) State Newton's third law and give an example.

点开看答案Show answer

(a) 由牛顿第二定律 F = ma：(a) From Newton's second law F = ma:

a = F / m = 20 N / 5 kg = 4 m s-2

(b) 牛顿第三定律：物体 A 对 B 施力时，B 同时对 A 施加一个大小相等、方向相反、作用在同一直线上的力。例：用手推墙，墙也以同样大小的力反推手。(b) Newton's third law: when A exerts a force on B, B simultaneously exerts a force on A that is equal in size, opposite in direction and along the same line. Example: when you push a wall, the wall pushes back on you with an equal force.

4[6]

把 2 kg 的物体竖直举高 3 m（取 g = 9.8 m s-2）。A 2 kg object is lifted vertically through 3 m (take g = 9.8 m s-2).

(a) 求克服重力做的功。(a) Find the work done against gravity.

(b) 物体获得的重力势能是多少？(b) What gravitational potential energy does the object gain?

点开看答案Show answer

(a) 克服重力做功 W = mgh：(a) Work done against gravity W = mgh:

W = mgh = 2 × 9.8 × 3 = 58.8 J

(b) 重力势能增量等于克服重力做的功（不计其他损失），所以 ΔGPE = 58.8 J。(b) The gain in GPE equals the work done against gravity (no other losses), so ΔGPE = 58.8 J.

5[6]

一列声波的频率为 50 Hz，波速为 340 m/s。求它的波长。A sound wave has frequency 50 Hz and speed 340 m/s. Find its wavelength.

点开看答案Show answer

由波速公式 v = fλ，解出 λ = v / f：From the wave equation v = fλ, so λ = v / f:

λ = v / f = 340 / 50 = 6.8 m
6[8]

一个 4 Ω 的电阻两端加上 12 V 的电压。A potential difference of 12 V is applied across a 4 Ω resistor.

(a) 求通过它的电流。(a) Find the current through it.

(b) 求电阻消耗的功率。(b) Find the power dissipated by the resistor.

点开看答案Show answer

(a) 由欧姆定律 V = IR，I = V / R：(a) From Ohm's law V = IR, so I = V / R:

I = V / R = 12 / 4 = 3 A

(b) 功率 P = VI：(b) Power P = VI:

P = VI = 12 × 3 = 36 W
7[6]

有两个电阻，阻值各为 4 Ω。Two resistors each have resistance 4 Ω.

(a) 它们串联时的总电阻是多少？(a) What is their total resistance in series?

(b) 它们并联时的总电阻是多少？(b) What is their total resistance in parallel?

点开看答案Show answer

(a) 串联电阻直接相加：(a) Series resistances add directly:

R = 4 + 4 = 8 Ω

(b) 并联取倒数相加再取倒数：(b) For parallel, add reciprocals then invert:

1/R = 1/4 + 1/4 = 1/2 → R = 2 Ω
8[4]

写出应力（stress）和应变（strain）的定义，并给出各自的公式。Define stress and strain, and give the formula for each.

点开看答案Show answer

应力：物体单位横截面积上受到的力，单位是帕斯卡（Pa）。应变：物体伸长量与原长之比，是比值，没有单位。Stress is the force per unit cross-sectional area, measured in pascals (Pa). Strain is the extension divided by the original length — a ratio with no unit.

应力 σ = F / A （单位 Pa） 应变 ε = x / L （无单位）

1[8]

一个 3 kg 的球以 2 m/s 运动，撞上一个静止的 2 kg 球，两球粘在一起继续运动。A 3 kg ball moving at 2 m/s hits a stationary 2 kg ball, and the two stick together.

用动量守恒求碰撞后它们的共同速度。Use conservation of momentum to find their common velocity after the collision.

点开看答案Show answer

碰撞前后总动量守恒：m₁u₁ + m₂u₂ = (m₁ + m₂)v，其中 u₂ = 0：Total momentum is conserved: m₁u₁ + m₂u₂ = (m₁ + m₂)v, with u₂ = 0:

3 × 2 + 2 × 0 = (3 + 2) × v 6 = 5v v = 6 / 5 = 1.2 m s-1
2[6]

(a) 写出线速度 v 与角速度 ω 的关系式，并说明每个符号的单位。(a) Write the relationship between linear velocity v and angular velocity ω, giving the unit of each symbol.

(b) 半径 r = 2 m、角速度 ω = 3 rad/s 时，线速度是多少？(b) When r = 2 m and ω = 3 rad/s, what is the linear velocity?

(a) 线速度 = 半径 × 角速度：v = rω。v 单位 m/s，r 单位 m，ω 单位 rad/s。(a) Linear velocity = radius × angular velocity: v = rω. v is in m/s, r in m, ω in rad/s.

v = rω

(b) 代入数值：(b) Substituting:

v = rω = 2 × 3 = 6 m s-1
3[6]

(a) 写出点电荷 Q 在距离 r 处产生的电场强度公式。(a) Write the formula for the electric field strength at distance r from a point charge Q.

(b) 定性地说明：距离加倍时，场强变为原来的多少？(b) Qualitatively: if the distance doubles, what happens to the field strength?

点开看答案Show answer

(a) 点电荷场强公式，其中 k = 1/(4πε₀)：(a) Field strength of a point charge, with k = 1/(4πε₀):

E = kQ / r² k = 1 / (4πε₀)

(b) 场强与距离的平方成反比，所以距离加倍 → 场强变为原来的 1/4。(b) The field strength is inversely proportional to the square of the distance, so doubling the distance reduces it to one quarter.

4[6]

(a) 写出电容的定义式 C = Q/V，并说明每个符号的含义。(a) Write the defining equation C = Q/V and state the meaning of each symbol.

(b) 1 F（1 法拉）是什么意思？(b) What does 1 F (one farad) mean?

点开看答案Show answer

(a) C 是电容（单位 F），Q 是储存的电荷（单位 C），V 是两极板间电压（单位 V）。(a) C is capacitance (in F), Q is stored charge (in C), V is the voltage across the plates (in V).

C = Q / V 1 F = 1 C / 1 V

(b) 1 F 表示：两极板间加 1 V 电压时，能储存 1 C 的电荷。(b) 1 F means the capacitor stores 1 C of charge when 1 V is applied across its plates.

5[8]

(a) 写出电荷量为 q 的粒子以速度 v 垂直进入磁感应强度为 B 的磁场时所受的洛伦兹力。(a) Write the Lorentz force on a particle of charge q moving perpendicular to a magnetic field of flux density B at speed v.

(b) 说明如何用左手定则判断这个力的方向。(b) Explain how to use Fleming's left-hand rule to find the direction of this force.

点开看答案Show answer

(a) 洛伦兹力大小 F = Bqv（v 与 B 垂直时）。(a) The magnitude of the Lorentz force is F = Bqv (when v is perpendicular to B).

F = Bqv

(b) 左手定则：让磁感线穿过掌心，四指指向正电荷的速度方向，则拇指指向受力方向。若粒子带负电，力的方向与拇指相反。(b) Left-hand rule: point the field lines into the palm, point the fingers along the velocity of a positive charge, and the thumb gives the force direction. For a negative charge, the force is opposite to the thumb.

6[6]

(a) 写出光子能量公式，并说明每个符号的含义。(a) Write the photon energy formula and state the meaning of each symbol.

(b) 在光电效应中，电子要从金属表面逸出，光子能量必须满足什么条件？(b) In the photoelectric effect, what condition must the photon energy satisfy for an electron to escape the metal surface?

点开看答案Show answer

(a) 光子能量 E = hf，h 是普朗克常量，f 是光的频率。(a) Photon energy E = hf, where h is Planck's constant and f is the light frequency.

E = hf Ek = hf − φ

(b) 光子能量必须大于金属的功函数 φ（hf > φ），电子才能逸出；多余的能量变成电子的最大动能 Ek = hf − φ。(b) The photon energy must exceed the metal's work function φ (hf > φ); the surplus becomes the electron's maximum kinetic energy, Ek = hf − φ.

7[6]

(a) 定义半衰期。(a) Define half-life.

(b) 某同位素的半衰期为 10 天。80 g 这种同位素经过 30 天后还剩多少？(b) An isotope has a half-life of 10 days. How much of an 80 g sample remains after 30 days?

点开看答案Show answer

(a) 半衰期：放射性原子核的数量（或质量）衰变到原来一半所需的时间。(a) Half-life: the time taken for the number (or mass) of radioactive nuclei to fall to half its original value.

(b) 30 天 = 3 个半衰期，质量每次减半：(b) 30 days = 3 half-lives, so the mass halves each time:

30 / 10 = 3 个半衰期 80 → 40 → 20 → 10 g
8[4]

(a) 交流电的有效值（r.m.s.）与峰值 I₀ 有什么关系？(a) How is the r.m.s. value of an alternating current related to its peak value I₀?

(b) 这个有效值代表什么物理含义？(b) What does the r.m.s. value physically represent?

点开看答案Show answer

(a) 正弦交流电的有效值 = 峰值 ÷ √2：(a) For a sinusoidal current, the r.m.s. value equals the peak divided by √2:

Irms = I₀ / √2

(b) 有效值表示：与相同热效应的直流电流等效的电流值，常用于计算交流电的平均功率。(b) The r.m.s. value is the direct current that produces the same heating effect, and is used to calculate the average power of an alternating current.
