1 位移、速度、加速度：三个量先分清Displacement, velocity, acceleration Scalar vs vector标量与矢量
这一站要你会：分清位移/速度（矢量）与距离/速率（标量），说出速度与加速度的定义和单位。Goal: separate displacement/velocity (vectors) from distance/speed (scalars), and state the definitions and units of velocity and acceleration.
这三个词其实都在说同一件事的三个层次：位移是你「挪到了哪」（多远、朝哪）；速度是你「挪得有多快」（还带方向）；加速度是你「变快或变慢得多猛」。想象开车：里程表告诉你跑了多少路（距离/速率），而 GPS 的箭头告诉你朝哪开（位移/速度）；踩油门那一刻的推背感，就是加速度。These three words are three layers of the same story: displacement is "how far, in which direction"; velocity is "how fast, in which direction"; acceleration is "how quickly the velocity changes". Picture driving: the odometer shows distance/speed, the GPS arrow shows direction (displacement/velocity), and the push in your back when you hit the pedal is acceleration.
三个定义，一字不差记下来：Three definitions to learn exactly:
  • 速度 velocity = 位移 ÷ 时间，单位 m s⁻¹，是矢量（有方向）。Velocity = displacement ÷ time, unit m s⁻¹, a vector (has direction).
  • 速率 speed = 距离 ÷ 时间，是标量（只有大小）。Speed = distance ÷ time, a scalar (magnitude only).
  • 加速度 acceleration = 速度变化量 ÷ 时间，单位 m s⁻²，也是矢量；减速就是负的加速度。Acceleration = change in velocity ÷ time, unit m s⁻², a vector; deceleration is negative acceleration.
最容易混的坑：「速度为零」不等于「加速度为零」。竖直上抛的球到最高点那一瞬间，速度是 0，但加速度一直是 g（它马上要往下掉）。反过来，匀速直线运动的物体加速度为 0，速度却可能很大。The classic trap: "zero velocity" does not mean "zero acceleration". At the top of a vertical throw the velocity is 0, but the acceleration is still g (it is about to fall back). Conversely, a car cruising at constant speed has zero acceleration yet a large velocity.
🗣 考场英文怎么说：How to say it in the exam: Displacement is a vector quantity, so it has both magnitude and direction, whereas distance is a scalar and has magnitude only. If you walk a complete loop, your distance travelled is non-zero but your displacement is zero.
例题Example
一辆车以 20 m s⁻¹ 向东开了 30 s，然后以 10 m s⁻¹ 向西开了 20 s。求（a）总路程；（b）最终位移（取向东为正）。A car drives east at 20 m s⁻¹ for 30 s, then west at 10 m s⁻¹ for 20 s. Find (a) the total distance, (b) the final displacement (take east as positive).
① 第一段路程 = 20 × 30 = 600 m；第二段 = 10 × 20 = 200 m。① first leg = 20 × 30 = 600 m; second = 10 × 20 = 200 m. ② 总路程 = 600 + 200 = 800 m（距离不管方向，都加）。② total distance = 600 + 200 = 800 m (distance ignores direction, so just add). ③ 位移：向东为正，向西为负 → +600 + (−200) = +400 m，即最终在起点以东 400 m。③ displacement: east positive, west negative → +600 + (−200) = +400 m, i.e. 400 m east of the start.
练习Practice
路程只看大小相加，位移要带方向。Add magnitudes for distance; keep directions for displacement.

一个人先向北走 300 m，再向东走 400 m。（a）他总共走了多远？（b）他的最终位移（大小）是多少？（提示：勾股定理）A person walks 300 m north, then 400 m east. (a) What distance did they cover? (b) What is the magnitude of their displacement? (Hint: Pythagoras)

点开看答案Show answer
(a) 总路程 = 300 + 400 = 700 m。
(b) 位移是起点到终点的直线：√(300² + 400²) = √250000 = 500 m，方向为北偏东。 (a) distance = 300 + 400 = 700 m.
(b) displacement is the straight line: √(300² + 400²) = √250000 = 500 m, directed north-east.

2 五个 suvat 方程（匀加速运动）The five suvat equations SUVATSUVAT
这一站要你会：记住五个 suvat 方程，并理解它们只在加速度恒定时成立。Goal: learn the five suvat equations and understand they only hold when acceleration is constant.
这五个公式看着多，其实是一家人：只要「加速度不变」，位移、初速度、末速度、加速度、时间这五个量就被死死绑在一起——你报出任意三个，另外两个就自动定死了。就像知道了三角形的三条边能推出所有角一样。别死背，理解每个公式「少了哪个字母」，选公式就快多了。These five formulas are one family: as long as acceleration is constant, the five quantities are locked together — state any three and the other two are forced. It is like knowing three sides of a triangle fixes all the angles. Don't memorise blindly; instead notice which letter each formula leaves out, and choosing becomes quick.
五个方程（注意每个「缺」了谁）：The five equations (note which one is missing):
① 没有 s： v = u + at ② 没有 a： s = (u + v)t / 2 ③ 没有 v： s = ut + ½at² ④ 没有 u： s = vt − ½at² ⑤ 没有 t： v² = u² + 2as
  • 单位要统一：s 用 m、v/u 用 m s⁻¹、a 用 m s⁻²、t 用 s。给的是 km、km h⁻¹ 要先换算。Keep units consistent: s in m, v/u in m s⁻¹, a in m s⁻², t in s. Convert km and km h⁻¹ first.
  • 方向决定正负：先规定正方向，与它相反的量带负号（比如向西、向下、减速）。Direction sets the sign: fix a positive direction; anything opposite (west, down, deceleration) is negative.
前提别忘：suvat 只在加速度恒定时能用。如果题目说「匀加速」「匀减速」「自由落体」就放心用；如果说加速度在变，就得用微积分那套（数学站单元 7、10）。Don't forget the condition: suvat only works when acceleration is constant. If the question says "uniform acceleration", "uniform deceleration" or "free fall", use it freely; if the acceleration is changing you need calculus (Maths site units 7 and 10).
🗣 考场英文怎么说：How to say it in the exam: The equations of motion (SUVAT) can only be used when the acceleration is constant. They relate the five quantities displacement, initial velocity, final velocity, acceleration and time.
例题Example
一辆车从静止（u = 0）以 2 m s⁻² 匀加速 10 s。求（a）末速度 v；（b）走过的位移 s。A car starts from rest (u = 0) and accelerates uniformly at 2 m s⁻² for 10 s. Find (a) the final velocity v, (b) the displacement s.
① 已知 u=0, a=2, t=10；求 v → 用① v = u + at。① known u=0, a=2, t=10; find v → use ① v = u + at. ② v = 0 + 2 × 10 = 20 m s⁻¹。② v = 0 + 2 × 10 = 20 m s⁻¹. ③ 求 s，可用③ s = ut + ½at² = 0 + ½×2×100 = 100 m。③ for s, use ③ s = ut + ½at² = 0 + ½×2×100 = 100 m.
练习Practice
先写下已知的四个量，看缺哪个，再选方程。Write the knowns, see which letter is missing, then pick the equation.

一物体以 5 m s⁻¹ 的初速度，匀加速到 25 m s⁻¹，加速度 4 m s⁻²。求它走了多远。A body accelerates uniformly from 5 m s⁻¹ to 25 m s⁻¹ at 4 m s⁻². Find how far it travels.

点开看答案Show answer
已知 u=5, v=25, a=4，求 s（缺 t）→ 用⑤ v² = u² + 2as。
25² = 5² + 2×4×s → 625 = 25 + 8s → 8s = 600 → s = 75 m。 Known u=5, v=25, a=4, find s (t missing) → use ⑤ v² = u² + 2as.
25² = 5² + 2×4×s → 625 = 25 + 8s → 8s = 600 → s = 75 m.

3 怎么选方程：已知什么、求什么Choosing the right equation Picking SUVAT选方程
这一站要你会：拿到题目先列五个量，把已知填上、未知画问号，然后「缺谁就避开谁」选出方程。Goal: list the five quantities, fill in the knowns, mark the unknown, then pick the formula that avoids the missing letter.
选公式不是靠背，是靠做减法。题目永远只给你五个量里的三个（有时藏在「从静止」= u=0、「停下」= v=0、「自由落体」= a=g 这些暗号里），再让你求第四个。你只要问一句：「哪个字母从头到尾没出现过？」——那个字母被排除，剩下的公式就出来了。Picking a formula is subtraction, not memorisation. The question always gives you three of the five quantities (sometimes hidden in code: "from rest" = u = 0, "comes to rest" = v = 0, "free fall" = a = g), and asks for a fourth. Just ask: "which letter never appears?" — exclude it and the formula is left standing.
藏在文字里的已知量（题目的暗号）：Knowns hidden in the wording:
  • 「从静止出发 / from rest」 → u = 0。"from rest" → u = 0.
  • 「停下 / comes to rest / stops」 → v = 0。"comes to rest" → v = 0.
  • 「自由落体 / 只受重力」 → a = g = 9.8 m s⁻²（竖直方向）。"free fall" → a = g = 9.8 m s⁻² (vertical).
  • 「匀减速 / decelerates」 → a 取负值。"decelerates" → a is negative.
方向陷阱：选完方程代入时，凡是与规定正方向相反的量都要带负号。上抛时如果取「向上为正」，g 就是 −9.8；取「向下为正」，g 就是 +9.8，但初速度 u 变负。两种取法都对，但全程只能用一个正方向。The sign trap: any quantity opposite to your chosen positive direction is negative. For a throw, taking up as positive makes g = −9.8; taking down as positive makes u negative instead. Both are fine, but stick to one positive direction throughout.
🗣 考场英文怎么说：How to say it in the exam: First identify which three of the five quantities are given, and which one is required. Then choose the equation of motion that does not contain the missing quantity.
例题Example
一列火车以 30 m s⁻¹ 匀速行驶，司机发现前方有障碍，刹车后以 3 m s⁻² 匀减速。求它从刹车到停下的滑行距离。A train travels at 30 m s⁻¹, then brakes with uniform deceleration 3 m s⁻². Find the distance it skids before stopping.
① 列量：u=30, v=0（停下）, a=−3（减速取负）, 求 s（没给 t）。① list: u=30, v=0 (stops), a=−3 (deceleration is negative), find s (no t). ② 缺 t → 用⑤ v² = u² + 2as。② t missing → use ⑤ v² = u² + 2as. ③ 0 = 30² + 2×(−3)×s → 0 = 900 − 6s → s = 150 m。③ 0 = 30² + 2×(−3)×s → 0 = 900 − 6s → s = 150 m.
练习Practice
「从静止」和「停下」都是暗号。"from rest" and "comes to rest" are clues.

一物体从静止出发，以 2 m s⁻² 匀加速，走了 100 m。求它的末速度。A body starts from rest and accelerates uniformly at 2 m s⁻² over 100 m. Find its final velocity.

点开看答案Show answer
已知 u=0, a=2, s=100，求 v（缺 t）→ 用⑤ v² = u² + 2as。
v² = 0 + 2×2×100 = 400 → v = 20 m s⁻¹。 Known u=0, a=2, s=100, find v (no t) → use ⑤ v² = u² + 2as.
v² = 0 + 2×2×100 = 400 → v = 20 m s⁻¹.

4 自由落体与竖直上抛（重力加速度 g）Free fall and vertical motion (g) Gravity重力
这一站要你会：把竖直运动当成「一维直线运动」来套 suvat，正确处理 g 的正负号。Goal: treat vertical motion as one-dimensional and apply suvat, handling the sign of g correctly.
竖直方向其实更简单：只要忽略空气阻力，一切下落的物体加速度都一样——g = 9.8 m s⁻²，方向竖直向下，跟它多重、往哪飞都无关。上抛和下落是同一套方程，唯一的坑是正方向：取「向上为正」，g 就写 −9.8；取「向下为正」，g 就写 +9.8。只要全程坚持一个方向，答案一定对。Vertically it is actually simpler: ignoring air resistance, everything falls with the same acceleration — g = 9.8 m s⁻² straight down, no matter its mass or how it was thrown. Throwing up and falling down use the same equations; the only trap is the sign: take up as positive and g = −9.8, or down as positive and g = +9.8. Stay consistent and the answer comes out right.
  • g = 9.8 m s⁻²（题目若说明取 10 就用 10），方向始终竖直向下。g = 9.8 m s⁻² (use 10 if the paper says so), always vertically downward.
  • 最高点 v = 0：这是上抛题里最常用的暗号，用它能求出上升到顶的时间或最大高度。At the top v = 0: the most useful clue in throw problems — it gives the time or height to the peak.
  • 对称性：理想情况下，上升到最高点的时间 = 从最高点落回原高度的时间；回到原高度时速率等于初速率（方向相反）。Symmetry: ideally, time up = time down to the same height, and the return speed equals the launch speed (opposite direction).
位移的符号会说话：上抛后某时刻算出的 s 若为负，说明它已经落到出发点以下了。别看到负位移就慌，那只是「位置在起点下方」的意思。Signs tell you where things are: if a displacement s comes out negative after a throw, the object is below its launch point. A negative displacement just means "below the start", nothing more alarming.
🗣 考场英文怎么说：How to say it in the exam: Neglecting air resistance, a body moving vertically has a constant downward acceleration of 9.8 m s⁻², equal to the acceleration of free fall, g. At its highest point its velocity is momentarily zero but its acceleration is still g.
例题Example
一个球以 19.6 m s⁻¹ 竖直向上抛出（取 g = 9.8 m s⁻²，向上为正）。求（a）它上升到最高点的时间；（b）最大高度。A ball is thrown vertically up at 19.6 m s⁻¹ (g = 9.8 m s⁻², up positive). Find (a) the time to reach the top, (b) the maximum height.
① 列量：u = +19.6, a = −9.8（向上为正，g 向下取负）。① list: u = +19.6, a = −9.8 (up positive, so g is negative). ② 最高点 v = 0，求 t → 用① v = u + at → 0 = 19.6 − 9.8t → t = 2.0 s。② at the top v = 0; find t → ① v = u + at → 0 = 19.6 − 9.8t → t = 2.0 s. ③ 求最大高度 s → 用③ s = ut + ½at² = 19.6×2 + ½×(−9.8)×4 = 39.2 − 19.6 = 19.6 m。③ max height s → ③ s = ut + ½at² = 19.6×2 + ½×(−9.8)×4 = 39.2 − 19.6 = 19.6 m.
练习Practice
最高点 v=0；注意 g 的符号。v = 0 at the top; watch the sign of g.

一块石头从 45 m 高的塔顶自由释放（初速度为 0），取 g = 10 m s⁻²，向下为正。求它落地所需的时间和落地速度。A stone is dropped from a 45 m tower (initial velocity 0). Take g = 10 m s⁻², down positive. Find the time to hit the ground and its impact speed.

求 t：用③ s = ut + ½at² → 45 = 0 + ½×10×t² = 5t² → t² = 9 → t = 3.0 s。
求 v：用① v = u + at = 0 + 10×3 = 30 m s⁻¹。 Known u=0, a=+10 (down positive), s=45; find t and v.
For t: ③ s = ut + ½at² → 45 = 0 + ½×10×t² = 5t² → t² = 9 → t = 3.0 s.
For v: ① v = u + at = 0 + 10×3 = 30 m s⁻¹.

5 刹车与匀减速（a 取负）Braking and deceleration Negative a负加速度
这一站要你会：识别「减速」= 负加速度，正确代入 suvat，并会算刹车距离、刹车时间这类问题。Goal: recognise deceleration as negative acceleration, apply it in suvat, and solve braking-distance and braking-time problems.
「减速」其实不是新东西，就是加速度和速度方向相反。速度向东、加速度向西，那车就在慢下来。公式一个都不用改——你只要在代入时把 a 写成负数。多数刹车题都藏着两个暗号：「停下」= v = 0，「匀减速 a m s⁻²」= 加速度 −a。找到它们，剩下就是代公式。Deceleration is nothing new — it is just acceleration pointing against velocity. Velocity east and acceleration west means the car is slowing. No formula changes; you simply write a as negative. Most braking questions hide two clues: "stops" = v = 0, and "decelerates at a m s⁻²" = acceleration −a. Spot them and it's just substitution.
  • 减速的两种说法：「以 3 m s⁻² 减速」= a = −3 m s⁻²。「减速度是 3」说的也是这个。Two ways to say it: "decelerates at 3 m s⁻²" = a = −3 m s⁻². "The deceleration is 3" means the same.
  • 刹车题三件套：初速度 u → 刹车减速度 a(负) → 停下 v=0。给了两个，求第三个（通常是 s 或 t）。The braking trio: initial velocity u → deceleration a (negative) → stop v=0. Given two, find the third (usually s or t).
  • 刹车距离与 v² 成正比：由 v² = u² + 2as 令 v=0 得 s = u²/(2|a|)。速度翻倍，刹车距离翻四倍！这就是「十次事故九次快」的物理原因。Stopping distance ∝ v²: from v² = u² + 2as with v = 0, s = u²/(2|a|). Double the speed and the distance quadruples! That is the physics behind "speed kills".
陷阱：题目给的是「减速度 = 3」还是「加速度 = −3」，其实一回事；但如果你把「减速度 3」直接当 +3 代进去，就会算出车越开越快的荒唐结果。代入前先问一句：这个 a 是让车变快还是变慢？Trap: "deceleration = 3" and "acceleration = −3" are the same thing; but if you plug "deceleration 3" in as +3 you'll get the absurd result that the car speeds up. Before substituting, ask: does this a make it faster or slower?
🗣 考场英文怎么说：How to say it in the exam: When a vehicle brakes with uniform deceleration, its acceleration is negative (opposite to its direction of motion), and at the moment it stops its final velocity is zero.

6 多段运动：先加速后匀速Two-stage motion Multi-stage多段运动
这一站要你会：把「先加速、再匀速、后减速」这类复合运动拆成几段，逐段求解并正确相加。Goal: split compound motion (accelerate → cruise → brake) into stages, solve each, and add correctly.
真实的旅程很少是「从头到尾一个加速度」——地铁先加速、再匀速、最后减速进站。但别慌：加速度只在段与段之间变，每一段内部还是匀加速。所以你就拿把剪刀，在加速度改变的地方剪开，每一段老老实实套一遍 suvat。唯一要记住的是接口：上一段的末速度，就是下一段的初速度——它像接力棒一样传下去。Real journeys rarely have one acceleration all the way — a metro speeds up, cruises, then brakes into the station. Don't panic: acceleration only changes between stages; within each stage it is constant. So take scissors, cut at every point where acceleration changes, and run suvat on each piece. The only thing to remember is the join: the final velocity of one stage is the initial velocity of the next — it passes on like a baton.
  • 分段标准：加速度一变就是新的一段（加速/匀速/减速各一段）。Where to cut: every change in acceleration starts a new stage.
  • 接口规则：上一段的 v = 下一段的 u；总位移 = 各段 s 之和；总时间 = 各段 t 之和。Join rules: previous v = next u; total s = sum of s's; total t = sum of t's.
  • 匀速段 a = 0：此时 suvat 退化成 s = vt（速度不变，位移 = 速度 × 时间）。Cruising stage a = 0: then suvat collapses to s = vt (constant speed, displacement = velocity × time).
别乱用一个 a 走全程：如果三段加速度不同，却拿第一段的 a 套全程，算出的一定错。宁可多写几行，一段一段来，也不要图省事。Never drag one a through the whole journey: if the three stages have different accelerations, using the first stage's a for everything is wrong. Better to write a few extra lines, stage by stage, than to cut corners.

斜率 = Δs / Δt = 速度 v
读图三件事：Three things to read:
  • 横轴是时间 t、纵轴是位移 s：图上一点 (t, s) 就是「在 t 时刻，物体在 s 位置」。Time t on the horizontal, displacement s on the vertical: a point (t, s) means "at time t, the object is at position s".

4 加速度-时间图与三张图的关系Acceleration–time graphs three graphs三图关系
这一站要你会：认识加速度-时间图（水平线 = 匀加速），并用「求导往下、积分往上」串起 s-t、v-t、a-t 三张图。Goal: recognise the acceleration–time graph (a horizontal line = constant acceleration) and link s–t, v–t and a–t through "differentiate down, integrate up".
三张图其实是祖孙三代：位移-时间图求导（看斜率）得到速度-时间图，速度-时间图再求导得到加速度-时间图；反过来，加速度-时间图积分（看面积）得到速度-时间图，再积分得到位移-时间图。听着吓人，其实就是两句话：往下是求导（斜率），往上是积分（面积）。你在单元 1 学的 suvat，到这里变成了「切一刀（斜率）」和「数格子（面积）」。The three graphs are three generations of one family: differentiate the displacement–time graph (take its slope) and you get the velocity–time graph; differentiate that and you get acceleration–time. In reverse, integrate the acceleration–time graph (take its area) to get velocity–time, and again for displacement–time. Sounds scary but it is two sentences: down is differentiate (slope), up is integrate (area). The suvat from Unit 1 turns into "cut a slope" and "count the area".

🗣 考场英文怎么说：How to say it in the exam: The three motion graphs are linked by calculus: the gradient of a displacement–time graph gives velocity, and the gradient of a velocity–time graph gives acceleration; conversely, the area under a velocity–time graph gives displacement, and the area under an acceleration–time graph gives the change in velocity.

5 从图像信息做综合题Reading graphs reading graphs读图综合
这一站要你会：拿到一张 v-t 图，算出总位移、平均速度，以及任意一段的加速度。Goal: given a velocity–time graph, find the total displacement, the average velocity, and the acceleration of any one section.
综合题的套路永远是三步：先看清楚图上有几段、每段是加速、匀速还是减速；然后三个问题轮着来——总位移（算总面积）、平均速度（总位移 ÷ 总时间）、某段加速度（那段直线的斜率）。平均速度别跟「速度的平均」搞混——只有匀变速时它俩才碰巧相等，所以老老实实用「总位移 ÷ 总时间」。The routine is always three steps: first see how many stages there are and whether each is speeding up, cruising or slowing down; then three questions come in turn — total displacement (the total area), average velocity (total displacement ÷ total time), and a stage's acceleration (the slope of that straight piece). Don't confuse average velocity with "the average of the speeds" — they only coincide for uniform acceleration, so just use displacement ÷ time.
总位移 = 各段面积之和 平均速度 = 总位移 ÷ 总时间
三个待求量的读法：How to read the three quantities:
  • 总位移：把 v-t 图下方切成三角形和矩形，面积相加。Total displacement: cut the area under the v–t graph into triangles and rectangles, and add them.
  • 平均速度：总位移 ÷ 总时间（不是「快慢的平均」，除非匀变速）。Average velocity: total displacement ÷ total time (not "the average of the speeds", unless uniformly accelerated).
  • 某段加速度：那一段直线的斜率 = 速度变化 ÷ 时间。A section's acceleration: the slope of that straight piece = change in velocity ÷ time.
平均速度的坑：车先快后慢时，平均速度不是「快速度 + 慢速度的一半」，而是总面积除以总时间。图上若出现「往回走」（线在横轴下方），位移要算负的，平均速度可能比你想的小，甚至为负。Average-velocity trap: if the car goes fast then slow, the average velocity is not "halfway between the fast and slow speeds" — it is total area ÷ total time. If the line dips below the axis (going back), that displacement is negative, so the average velocity can be smaller than expected, even negative.
🗣 考场英文怎么说：How to say it in the exam: From a velocity–time graph: the total displacement is the total area under the graph; the average velocity is the total displacement divided by the total time; the acceleration during any straight section is the gradient of that section.

1 力是什么：矢量、单位和三要素What is a force: vector, unit, three elements Vector & newtons矢量与牛顿
这一站要你会：说出力是矢量、单位是牛顿（N），认出力的大小、方向、作用点三要素，并能叫出几种常见力的名字。Goal: state that force is a vector measured in newtons (N), identify its three elements (magnitude, direction, point of application), and name the common forces.
力看不见摸不着，但你天天在和它打交道——推门、拎书包、把球踢飞，都是「力」在推或拉。力学里，力是一支有方向的箭：它有多大（大小）、朝哪使（方向）、顶在哪一点（作用点），三点缺一不可。就像你要说清「我推了桌子」，得说清使了多大力、往哪推、手按在桌子哪一块。You can't see a force, but you deal with them all day — pushing a door, lifting a bag, kicking a ball. In mechanics a force is a directional arrow: how big, which way, and where it presses. Like saying "I pushed the table" — you'd need how hard, which direction, and where your hand was.
先记下这几条：Remember these:
  • 力是矢量：有大小、有方向，单位是牛顿 N。Force is a vector: it has magnitude and direction, measured in newtons (N).
  • 三要素：大小、方向、作用点——少了任何一条，这个力就没说清。Three elements: magnitude, direction, point of application — miss one and the force is unspecified.
  • 常见力：重力 mg（竖直向下）、法向反作用力 R（垂直接触面向上）、拉力 T（沿绳/被拉方向）、摩擦力 F（阻碍运动）。Common forces: weight mg (straight down), normal reaction R (perpendicular to the surface), tension T (along the string), friction F (opposing motion).
矢量 vs 标量：力有方向，所以是矢量；质量、长度只有大小、没有方向，是标量。判断一个量是不是矢量，就问一句「它有没有方向」。Vector vs scalar: force has a direction, so it is a vector; mass and length have only a size, so they are scalars. Ask "does it have a direction?" to tell them apart.
🗣 考场英文怎么说：How to say it in the exam: Force is a vector quantity: it has magnitude, direction and a point of application, and it is measured in newtons (N).

2 力的合成：平行四边形与勾股定理Combining forces: parallelogram and Pythagoras Resultant force合力
这一站要你会：用平行四边形（或三角形）法则求两个力的合力；两力垂直时用勾股定理求大小、用正切求方向。Goal: find the resultant of two forces by the parallelogram (or triangle) law; when they are perpendicular, use Pythagoras for the size and tangent for the direction.
两个力同时作用在一个物体上，效果和一个「合成后的力」一模一样，这个新力就叫合力。想象两个人一起拉一辆车：一个往东拽、一个往北拽，车其实沿着东北方向走——那根「沿东北的箭头」就是合力。求合力就像拼三角形：两个力是两条边，合力是第三条边。Two forces acting together have the same effect as one "combined" force — the resultant. Picture two people pulling a cart: one east, one north, and the cart actually goes north-east. That north-east arrow is the resultant. Finding it is like completing a triangle: the two forces are two sides, the resultant is the third.
合成三件套：The three rules:
  • 平行四边形法则：以两力为邻边画平行四边形，对角线就是合力。Parallelogram law: draw a parallelogram with the two forces as sides; the diagonal is the resultant.
  • 三角形法则：把一个力平移，让它首尾相接，从起点到终点就是合力。Triangle law: slide one force so they join head-to-tail; the closing side is the resultant.
  • 两力垂直：合力大小 R = √(F₁² + F₂²)，方向 tanθ = F₂/F₁（θ 是合力与 F₁ 的夹角）。Perpendicular forces: magnitude R = √(F₁² + F₂²), direction tanθ = F₂/F₁ (θ measured from F₁).
前提别忘：只有两力垂直才能直接勾股。不垂直时要用余弦定理或先分解（第 3 站）。考试最爱考垂直情况，所以先把这一招练到闭眼都会。Don't forget: Pythagoras only works when the forces are perpendicular. Otherwise use the cosine rule or resolve first (stop 3). Exams love the perpendicular case, so drill this until it's automatic.
🗣 考场英文怎么说：How to say it in the exam: The resultant of two forces is found by the parallelogram or triangle of forces. When they are perpendicular, its magnitude is R = √(F₁² + F₂²) and its direction satisfies tanθ = F₂/F₁.

3 力的分解：F cosθ 与 F sinθResolving forces: F cosθ and F sinθ Resolving分解
这一站要你会：把一个力沿互相垂直的两个方向分解，熟练写出 F cosθ 和 F sinθ。Goal: resolve a force into two perpendicular directions and confidently write F cosθ and F sinθ.
合成是把几个力并成一个，分解就是反过来——把一个斜着的力拆成「水平 + 竖直」两个小力，它们合起来的效果完全一样。就像你斜着推箱子，其实等于「又往前推、又往下压」。记住两条腿：贴水平的叫水平分量（F cosθ），贴竖直的叫竖直分量（F sinθ）。Combining merges forces; resolving does the reverse — split one slanted force into "horizontal + vertical" parts that together have the same effect. Like pushing a box diagonally is really "pushing forward and pressing down" at once. The horizontal leg is the horizontal component (F cosθ), the vertical leg the vertical component (F sinθ).
分解口诀：Resolving rules:
  • 方向任选：最常用「水平 + 竖直」，斜面题用「沿斜面 + 垂直斜面」（第 6 站）。Pick your axes: usually horizontal + vertical; on slopes use along + perpendicular to the slope (stop 6).
  • F 与水平成 θ：水平分量 = F cosθ，竖直分量 = F sinθ。F at angle θ to the horizontal: horizontal component = F cosθ, vertical = F sinθ.
  • 角换个位置，cos/sin 就互换：θ 是「与水平面的夹角」时，水平用 cos；θ 是「与竖直方向的夹角」时，水平就换成 sin。If the angle moves, cos and sin swap: θ measured from the horizontal puts cos on the horizontal; θ from the vertical puts sin there instead.
别硬背谁配谁：拿 θ=0 试一下（力贴着水平）——竖直分量应该为 0：竖直 = F sin0 = 0 ✓，水平 = F cos0 = F ✓。用这个「贴水平」的直觉，永远错不了。Don't memorise blindly: test θ = 0 (the force lies flat) — the vertical part should vanish: F sin0 = 0 ✓, horizontal = F cos0 = F ✓. Use this flat-force intuition and you can't go wrong.
🗣 考场英文怎么说：How to say it in the exam: A force F at angle θ to the horizontal can be resolved into a horizontal component F cosθ and a vertical component F sinθ.

4 静力平衡：合力为零、两个方程Equilibrium: zero resultant, two equations Equilibrium平衡
这一站要你会：理解平衡 = 合力为零（静止或匀速），并用「水平合力 = 0、竖直合力 = 0」两个方程解出未知力。Goal: understand equilibrium as zero resultant (at rest or constant velocity), and solve for unknown forces using ΣFx = 0 and ΣFy = 0.
一个东西「纹丝不动」或「匀速直线滑行」，说明它身上的力全部抵消了——合力为零，这就叫平衡。就像拔河：两边势均力敌，绳子不动，不是因为没有力，而是两边的力刚好打平。解题就靠两条铁律：水平方向的力相加 = 0，竖直方向的力相加 = 0。If something stays perfectly still — or glides at constant velocity — its forces have cancelled out: the resultant is zero, called equilibrium. Like tug-of-war: the rope doesn't move not because there's no force, but because the two sides balance. Two iron rules solve it: horizontal forces add to 0, vertical forces add to 0.
平衡三句话：Equilibrium in three lines:
  • 平衡条件：合力 = 0 → 物体静止或匀速直线运动。Condition: resultant force = 0 → the body is at rest or moves with constant velocity.
  • 三力平衡：可画成闭合三角形（首尾相接绕一圈）。Three forces in balance: they form a closed triangle (head-to-tail, round the loop).
  • 两个方程：ΣFx = 0 和 ΣFy = 0。有几个未知量，就列几个方程。Two equations: ΣFx = 0 and ΣFy = 0. Write one equation per unknown.
最容易踩的坑：「静止」和「匀速直线」在力学里是同一件事——合力都是零。别一看到「匀速」就以为合力不为零、要算加速度。The classic trap: "at rest" and "constant velocity" are the same thing in mechanics — the resultant is zero in both. Don't see "constant velocity" and wrongly assume a non-zero force.

5 摩擦力：F = μRFriction: F = μR Friction摩擦
这一站要你会：理解摩擦力阻碍运动（或运动趋势），会用 F = μR 计算最大静摩擦。Goal: understand that friction opposes motion (or its tendency) and compute maximum static friction with F = μR.
摩擦是「表面之间的倔强」——你想让箱子滑动，地面偏要拉住它不让动，这个拉住你的力就是摩擦力。它有个上限：最大静摩擦 F = μR。μ 是摩擦系数（表面越粗糙越大），R 是法向反作用力（压得越紧，摩擦越大）。一旦推力超过这个上限，物体就开始动了。Friction is the stubbornness between two surfaces — try to slide a box and the floor pulls back. That pull is friction. It has a ceiling: maximum static friction F = μR. μ is the coefficient (rougher = bigger), R is the normal reaction (harder press = more friction). Push past this ceiling and the object starts to move.
摩擦三条：Three friction facts:
  • 方向：摩擦阻碍运动或运动趋势，方向与（趋势的）运动方向相反。Direction: friction opposes motion or its tendency, pointing against the (would-be) motion.
  • 最大静摩擦：F = μR（μ 摩擦系数，R 法向反作用力）。Maximum static friction: F = μR (μ coefficient, R normal reaction).
  • 动摩擦也用 F = μR，但动摩擦系数通常略小于静摩擦系数。Kinetic friction also uses F = μR, with a slightly smaller coefficient than static.
R 不总是等于重力！只有在水平面上 R = mg。在斜面上 R = mg cosθ（下一站就讲）。别一见到 R 就条件反射写 mg。R is not always mg! Only on a horizontal surface does R = mg. On a slope R = mg cosθ (next stop). Don't reflexively write mg whenever you see R.
🗣 考场英文怎么说：How to say it in the exam: Friction opposes motion or the tendency to move. The maximum friction is F = μR, where μ is the coefficient of friction and R is the normal reaction force.

① 水平面上 R = mg = 10 × 9.8 = 98 N。① on horizontal ground R = mg = 10 × 9.8 = 98 N. ② 最大静摩擦 F = μR = 0.4 × 98 = 39.2 N。② max friction F = μR = 0.4 × 98 = 39.2 N.

6 斜面平衡：mg sinθ 与 mg cosθEquilibrium on a slope: mg sinθ and mg cosθ Inclined plane斜面
这一站要你会：把斜面上的重力分解成 mg sinθ 与 mg cosθ，并求出物体静止时的摩擦力和支持力。Goal: resolve the weight on a slope into mg sinθ and mg cosθ, and find the friction and normal reaction when the object is at rest.
斜面是摩擦力的「主场」——重力竖直向下，但斜面只在乎「压着它的」和「想让它滑下去的」两部分。把重力拆开：沿斜面往下拽的那份叫 mg sinθ，垂直压着斜面的那份叫 mg cosθ。物体静止时，摩擦力正好顶住 mg sinθ，支持力正好顶住 mg cosθ。Slopes are friction's home turf — weight points straight down, but the slope only cares about the part pressing into it and the part trying to slide down. Split the weight: mg sinθ down the slope, and mg cosθ into the slope. At rest, friction balances mg sinθ and the normal reaction balances mg cosθ.

  • 重力分解：沿斜面 = mg sinθ，垂直斜面 = mg cosθ。Resolve the weight: down the slope = mg sinθ, perpendicular = mg cosθ.
  • 静止在斜面上：摩擦力 f = mg sinθ，支持力 R = mg cosθ。At rest on the slope: friction f = mg sinθ, normal reaction R = mg cosθ.
  • θ 越大，mg sinθ 越大 → 越容易滑下来。Bigger θ → bigger mg sinθ → easier to slide down.
哪份是 sin、哪份是 cos？拿 θ = 0 试：水平时重力全压在面上、没有下滑分量 → 沿斜面 mg sin0 = 0 ✓，垂直斜面 mg cos0 = mg ✓。记住「θ=0 时 sin 那份消失」，就永远分得清。Which is sin, which is cos? Test θ = 0: on flat ground the whole weight presses in and nothing slides → mg sin0 = 0 ✓, mg cos0 = mg ✓. Remember "the sin part vanishes at θ = 0" and you'll never mix them up.

1 牛顿第一定律：惯性Newton's first law: inertia Inertia惯性
这一站要你会：说出牛顿第一定律的内容，理解「合力为零」意味着速度不变，并知道惯性只跟质量有关。Goal: state Newton's first law, see that zero resultant force means unchanged velocity, and know that inertia depends only on mass.
想象冰面上那个冰球：你推它一下，它自己就滑出去好远，没人继续推它，它也不停。为什么？因为几乎没有摩擦力去改变它。牛顿第一定律说的就是这件事：没有外力去改变它，物体就保持原来的样子——原来静止就静止，原来运动就一直匀速直线运动。这种「保持原样」的懒劲儿，就叫惯性。东西越重，惯性越大，越难被推得动、也越难被拉得停。Picture a puck on ice: give it one push and it glides far, with no one pushing — yet it doesn't stop. Why? Because almost no friction changes it. Newton's first law says exactly this: with no outside force, a body stays as it was — at rest if it was at rest, or moving in a straight line if it was moving. This "staying as it is" laziness is called inertia. The heavier the object, the bigger its inertia — harder to start and harder to stop.
第一定律，一句说清：The first law in one line:
  • 牛顿第一定律：物体所受合力为零时，静止的保持静止，运动的保持匀速直线运动。Newton's first law: with a zero resultant force, a body at rest stays at rest and a moving body keeps moving with constant velocity in a straight line.
  • 惯性：物体抵抗运动状态改变的性质，只与质量有关——质量越大，惯性越大。Inertia: the tendency to resist change in motion; it depends only on mass — more mass, more inertia.
  • 「合力为零」≠「没有力」：桌上的书，重力向下、桌面支持力向上，两个力抵消，合力才是零。"Zero resultant" ≠ "no forces": a book on a table has gravity down and the table's support up — they cancel, so the resultant is zero.
最容易混的坑：「速度为零」不等于「合力为零」。车急刹停下的那一瞬间，速度是 0，但合力很大（摩擦力死死拖住它）；反过来，匀速行驶时速度不是 0，合力却是 0。第一定律说的是「合力为零 → 速度不变」，不是「速度为零」。The classic trap: "zero velocity" is not "zero resultant force". The instant a car stops, its velocity is 0 yet the resultant force is large (friction holds it hard); while cruising, velocity is not 0 yet the resultant is 0. The law says "zero resultant → unchanged velocity", not "zero velocity".
🗣 考场英文怎么说：How to say it in the exam: Newton's first law states that a body remains at rest or moves with constant velocity in a straight line unless a resultant force acts on it. This tendency to resist change in motion is called inertia, and it depends only on the mass of the body.

2 牛顿第二定律 F = maNewton's second law: F = ma F = ma牛顿第二定律
这一站要你会：说出 F = ma 的含义，会先求合力再用 a = F/m 求加速度，并正确使用单位。Goal: state the meaning of F = ma, find the resultant force first, then use a = F/m, with correct units.
F = ma 是整门力学的心脏：它把「力」和「动」连了起来——合力越大、物体越轻，加速度就越大。想象推购物车：空车轻轻一推就滑走，装满东西的车要使出吃奶的劲儿才推得动——同样的力，质量大了，加速度就小。这就是 F = ma 在生活里的样子。记住一个铁律：式子里的 F 是合力（所有力矢量合成后的净力），不是随便哪一个力。F = ma is the heart of mechanics: it links "force" and "motion" — bigger resultant force and lighter mass mean bigger acceleration. Think of a trolley: an empty one glides away with a gentle push, a loaded one needs all your strength — same force, more mass, less acceleration. That is F = ma in real life. One iron rule: the F here is the resultant (all forces added as vectors), not just any single force.
核心方程 + 单位：The core equation and its units:
F = ma 1 N = 1 kg m s⁻²
  • F 是合力：先画受力图，把所有力（带方向）矢量相加，得到的净力才代入公式。F is the resultant: draw the force diagram, add all forces (with direction) as vectors, and only the net force goes into the formula.
  • 单位定义：1 N（牛顿）就是使 1 kg 物体产生 1 m s⁻² 加速度的力。Unit definition: 1 N (newton) is the force that gives a 1 kg mass an acceleration of 1 m s⁻².
  • 方向一致：加速度的方向永远与合力的方向相同。Same direction: acceleration always points in the same direction as the resultant force.
新手最容易错：拿某一个力直接除以质量。比如物体同时受向右 20 N 和向左 5 N 两个力，有人直接 a = 20/m，就错了——得先合成：合力 = 20 − 5 = 15 N，再 a = 15/m。The beginner's mistake: dividing by one single force instead of the resultant. If a body has 20 N right and 5 N left, don't write a = 20/m — combine first: resultant = 20 − 5 = 15 N, then a = 15/m.

3 牛顿第三定律：作用与反作用Newton's third law: action and reaction Action–reaction作用与反作用
这一站要你会：说出第三定律的内容，理解一对力等大反向但作用在不同物体上，并能举出生活里的例子。Goal: state the third law, understand that the pair is equal and opposite but acts on different objects, and give real-life examples.
你用力推墙，墙其实也在用同样大的力推你——只是墙没动，你的手先疼了。这就是第三定律：每一个作用力，都有一个大小相等、方向相反的反作用力。最容易忽略、也最要命的一点是：这对力作用在不同的物体上。所以它们永远抵消不了。火箭能飞，靠的就是这个——发动机把气体用力往后喷，气体就用力往前推火箭。你走路，也是脚往后蹬地、地往前推你，人才向前走。Push a wall hard and the wall pushes you back just as hard — the wall doesn't move, but your hand hurts first. That is the third law: every action has an equal and opposite reaction. The point most people miss, and the one that matters most, is that the two forces act on different objects. So they never cancel. A rocket flies on exactly this — the engine blasts gas backwards and the gas pushes the rocket forwards. When you walk, your foot pushes the ground back and the ground pushes you forward.
第三定律，三条要记牢：Three things to remember:
  • 内容：作用力与反作用力大小相等、方向相反，作用在不同物体上。The law: action and reaction are equal in size, opposite in direction, and act on different objects.
  • 例子：人推墙、火箭喷气、走路蹬地，都是这一对力。Examples: pushing a wall, rocket thrust, walking — all action–reaction pairs.
  • 关键区别：这对力不抵消（不同物体）；而「平衡力」作用在同一物体上，才会抵消。The key difference: this pair never cancels (different objects); only "balanced forces" on the same object cancel.
别把「作用反作用」和「平衡力」搞混：书放在桌上，书的重力（地球拉书）和桌对书的支持力作用在同一个物体（书）上，是一对平衡力；而书压桌的力和桌支持书的力，分别作用在桌和书两个物体上，才是作用与反作用力。Don't confuse "action–reaction" with "balanced forces": a book on a table — gravity (Earth pulls book) and the table's support both act on the same object (the book), so they are balanced forces; but the book pressing the table and the table supporting the book act on two different objects, so they are an action–reaction pair.
🗣 考场英文怎么说：How to say it in the exam: Newton's third law states that for every action there is an equal and opposite reaction. The two forces act on different objects, so they never cancel each other out.

物体放在斜面上为什么会自己滑下来？因为重力不是「正对着斜面压下去」，而是斜着往下拉。聪明的做法是把它拆成两份：一份沿斜面往下（mg sinθ），负责让物体下滑；一份垂直压进斜面（mg cosθ），被斜面的支持力顶掉。剩下的就只有那份沿斜面的力在干活了。所以斜面越陡，θ 越大，sinθ 越大，下滑就越快；当 θ = 90°，斜面竖起来了，sinθ = 1，a = g——又变回自由落体，这个「极端检查」能帮你牢牢记住公式。Why does a body slide down a slope on its own? Because gravity doesn't press straight into the slope — it pulls at an angle. The smart move is to split it in two: one part down the slope (mg sinθ) that makes it slide, and one part pressing into the slope (mg cosθ) that the support cancels out. All that's left doing the work is the down-slope part. So the steeper the slope, the bigger θ, the bigger sinθ, the faster it slides; at θ = 90° the slope is vertical, sinθ = 1, a = g — back to free fall. This "extreme check" helps you remember the formula for good.
斜面公式：The slope formulas:
a = g sinθ a = g sinθ − μg cosθ
  • 沿斜面分量 = mg sinθ：使物体下滑的力。Down-slope component = mg sinθ: the force that pulls it down.
  • 垂直斜面分量 = mg cosθ：被支持力平衡掉。Perpendicular component = mg cosθ: balanced by the support force.
  • 无摩擦（光滑）：a = g sinθ；有摩擦：a = g sinθ − μg cosθ。Smooth: a = g sinθ; with friction: a = g sinθ − μg cosθ.
摩擦项怎么来的：摩擦力 f = μN，而垂直斜面的支持力 N = mg cosθ，所以 f = μmg cosθ。它跟下滑方向相反，于是加速度里要减掉 μg cosθ。Where the friction term comes from: friction f = μN, and the support N = mg cosθ, so f = μmg cosθ. It opposes the motion, so we subtract μg cosθ from the acceleration.
🗣 考场英文怎么说：How to say it in the exam: On a smooth inclined plane the component of the weight down the slope is mg sinθ, so the acceleration is a = g sinθ. With friction the acceleration becomes g sinθ − μg cosθ.
例题Example
一个光滑斜面与水平面成 30° 角。求木块沿斜面下滑的加速度（取 g = 10 m s⁻²）。A smooth plane is inclined at 30° to the horizontal. Find the acceleration of a block down the plane (take g = 10 m s⁻²).
① 光滑 → 无摩擦，直接用 a = g sinθ。① smooth → no friction, so use a = g sinθ. ② a = 10 × sin30° = 10 × 0.5 = 5 m s⁻²，沿斜面向下。② a = 10 × sin30° = 10 × 0.5 = 5 m s⁻², down the slope.

5 连接体：一根绳拴两个物Connected particles: one string, two bodies Connected particles连接体
这一站要你会：理解连接体「加速度相同、张力相同」，并对每个物体分别列 F = ma 联立求解。Goal: see that connected particles share the same acceleration and the same tension, then write F = ma for each body and solve together.
一根绳拴两个物体，它们就成了一个「共同体」：一个动，另一个必须跟着动，所以加速度 a 相同；绳子是轻的、不伸长，所以张力 T 处处相等。解这种题的套路很死板也很有用：对每个物体各列一条 F = ma（沿它自己的运动方向列），得到两条方程，再联立解出 a 和 T。记住别把两个物体当成一个「大块」硬套一条式子——分开列，式子干净，符号也不容易乱。Tie two bodies with one string and they become a team: when one moves the other must follow, so the acceleration a is the same; the string is light and doesn't stretch, so the tension T is the same everywhere. The routine is boring but powerful: write F = ma for each body (along its own direction of motion), get two equations, then solve them together for a and T. Don't force the two bodies into one "big block" equation — write them separately and the signs stay clean.
连接体三条规律：Three rules for connected particles:
  • 轻绳：质量忽略，张力 T 处处相等。Light string: its mass is ignored and the tension T is the same throughout.
  • 加速度相同：两个物体加速度大小一样。Same acceleration: both bodies have the same magnitude of acceleration.
  • 方法：对每个物体分别列 F = ma，联立求解。Method: write F = ma for each body and solve the equations together.
符号不乱的秘诀：先定一个「正方向」（让运动方向为正），每个物体都沿着它自己的运动方向列方程。比如悬挂的 B 向下运动，就对 B 取「向下为正」，重力为正、张力为负；桌上的 A 向右运动，就对 A 取「向右为正」，张力为正。The sign-fixing trick: fix a positive direction first (make the direction of motion positive), and write each body's equation along its own motion. If hanging B moves down, take down as positive for B — weight positive, tension negative; if A on the table moves right, take right as positive for A — tension positive.
🗣 考场英文怎么说：How to say it in the exam: For two particles joined by a light inextensible string, the tension is the same throughout the string and both particles have the same acceleration. Apply F = ma to each particle and solve the resulting equations together.
例题Example

① 对 B（向下为正）：mg − T = ma → 10 − T = 1a。① for B (down positive): mg − T = ma → 10 − T = 1a. ② 对 A（向右为正）：T = 3a。② for A (right positive): T = 3a. ③ 代入：10 − 3a = a → a = 2.5 m s⁻²。③ substitute: 10 − 3a = a → a = 2.5 m s⁻². ④ T = 3 × 2.5 = 7.5 N。④ T = 3 × 2.5 = 7.5 N.

6 电梯里的重量（视重）Lift problems: apparent weight Apparent weight视重
这一站要你会：用 R = m(g ± a) 判断电梯里视重的变化，并理解失重（a = g）时 R = 0。Goal: use R = m(g ± a) to judge how apparent weight changes in a lift, and see that R = 0 in free fall (a = g).
坐电梯加速上升的那一刻，是不是觉得脚底被往下一压，整个人「变重」了？这不是你真变重了，是地板（秤）对你的支持力 R 变大了。你感觉到的重量，其实是这个支持力，我们叫它视重。向上加速时，合力要向上，所以 R 得比 mg 大；向下加速时反过来，R 比 mg 小，人就觉得「变轻」；要是电梯的钢丝绳断了、自由下落，a = g，R 直接变成 0——这就是失重，人飘起来了。The instant a lift accelerates upwards, don't you feel your feet pressed down, your whole body "heavier"? You didn't actually get heavier — the floor (the scale) pushes you up with a bigger support force R. The weight you feel is really this support force, called your apparent weight. Accelerating up needs the resultant to point up, so R must exceed mg; accelerating down is the reverse, R is less than mg, and you feel "lighter". If the cable snaps and the lift falls freely, a = g and R drops to 0 — that is weightlessness, and you float.
视重公式（R 就是秤的读数）：The apparent-weight formulas (R is the scale reading):
R = m(g + a) R = m(g − a)
  • 向上加速：R = m(g + a)，感觉更重。Accelerating up: R = m(g + a), you feel heavier.
  • 向下加速：R = m(g − a)，感觉更轻。Accelerating down: R = m(g − a), you feel lighter.
  • 失重：a = g 时 R = 0（自由下落）。Weightlessness: when a = g, R = 0 (free fall).
记公式的窍门：看加速度的方向决定「加」还是「减」。向上加速 → g 上加 a（加重）；向下加速 → g 上减 a（减重）。别死背，先想「加速向上，合力向上，R 得比 mg 大」就推导出来了。The memory trick: let the direction of the acceleration decide "plus" or "minus". Accelerating up → add a to g (heavier); accelerating down → subtract a from g (lighter). Don't memorise — think "accelerating up means resultant up, so R must beat mg" and the formula derives itself.
🗣 考场英文怎么说：How to say it in the exam: In a lift, the normal reaction R equals m(g + a) when accelerating upwards and m(g − a) when accelerating downwards. If the lift falls freely, R = 0 and the person feels weightless.
例题Example

① 向上加速 → R = m(g + a)。① accelerating up → R = m(g + a). ② R = 70 × (9.8 + 2) = 70 × 11.8 = 826 N。② R = 70 × (9.8 + 2) = 70 × 11.8 = 826 N.

单元 4 小结Unit 4 summary

① 第一定律：合力为零 → 保持静止或匀速直线运动（惯性只与质量有关）。
② 第二定律：F = ma，F 是合力，单位 1 N = 1 kg m s⁻²——先合成再除质量。
③ 第三定律：作用与反作用等大反向，但作用在不同物体上，不抵消。

② Second law: F = ma, where F is the resultant, with 1 N = 1 kg m s⁻² — combine first, then divide by mass.
③ Third law: action and reaction are equal and opposite but act on different objects, so they never cancel.
④ slopes, connected particles and lifts are all F = ma in disguise: mg sinθ on a slope, one equation per body for connected particles, and R = m(g ± a) in a lift.

动量这个词，说白了就是「一个东西在动，它有多猛」。想象一个篮球和一个铅球，都用同样的速度朝你滚过来——你肯定更怕铅球，因为它重。再看同一个篮球，慢悠悠滚来和被大力抽射过来，你更怕哪个？肯定是快的。动量把这两件事合在一起：又重又快的东西，动量就大，想让它停下就越费劲。Momentum is simply "how much oomph a moving thing has". 动量三要素：Three things about momentum:
p = mv 单位：kg m s⁻¹（= N s）p = mv unit: kg m s⁻¹ (= N s)

2 冲量与动量变化：Ft = mv − muImpulse and change in momentum Impulse冲量
这一站要你会：理解冲量 = 力 × 时间，等于动量的变化 Ft = mv − mu，并会用「时间越短力越大」解释生活现象。Goal: understand impulse = force × time, equal to the change in momentum Ft = mv − mu, and explain "shorter time means bigger force".
接飞来的球，你下意识会把手往后缩一下，对吗？不缩，球「啪」一下停在你手上，力大、手疼；往后一缩，球是慢慢停下的，手就不疼了。其实球的动量变化一模一样（从有到无），区别只在于停下来的时间。力 × 时间这个量叫冲量，它等于动量的变化——时间拉长，力自然就小。安全气囊、拳击手套、跳远沙坑，全是同一个道理。When you catch a ball you instinctively let your hand give — right? Catch it stiff and the ball stops dead: big force, stinging hand. Let it give and it stops slowly: no pain. The ball's change in momentum is identical (from moving to stopped); the only difference is the stopping time. Force × time is called impulse, and it equals the change in momentum — stretch the time and the force shrinks. Airbags, boxing gloves, and long-jump sand pits are all the same idea.
两个核心：Two core ideas:
冲量 = 力 × 时间：F t 动量定理：F t = m v − m uimpulse = force × time: F t impulse = change in momentum: F t = m v − m u

  • 冲量的方向 = 动量变化的方向（与平均力同向）。The impulse's direction = direction of the momentum change (same as the average force).

3 动量守恒：碰撞前后总量不变Conservation of momentum Momentum conserved动量守恒
这一站要你会：理解动量守恒的条件（系统不受外力），会用 m₁u₁ + m₂u₂ = m₁v₁ + m₂v₂ 解碰撞题。Goal: understand the condition for conservation (no external force) and use m₁u₁ + m₂u₂ = m₁v₁ + m₂v₂ in collision problems.
打台球：白球撞上静止的彩球，白球慢下来，彩球却飞了出去。动量没有凭空消失，也没有凭空冒出——它只是从白球传给了彩球。只要没有外力插手（桌面摩擦忽略不计），碰撞前所有球的总动量，等于碰撞后的总动量。这就是动量守恒，它是解一切碰撞题的「总账本」。In snooker the white ball hits a stationary colour, slows down, and the colour shoots off. Momentum is not created or destroyed — it is handed over from the white to the colour. As long as no outside force interferes (ignore friction), the total momentum before equals the total after. That is conservation of momentum — the master ledger of every collision.

  • 套路：① 定正方向；② 写碰撞前总动量；③ 写碰撞后总动量；④ 令两者相等，解未知量。Recipe: ① fix a positive direction; ② write total momentum before; ③ write total after; ④ set them equal and solve.
  • 一个球静止时，它的那一项就是 0，别漏。If a ball is at rest, its term is 0 — don't drop it.

5 反冲与爆炸Recoil and explosions Recoil反冲
这一站要你会：理解反冲/爆炸中动量守恒（初总动量为 0），会算两部分的速度，知道它们方向相反。Goal: understand momentum conservation in recoil/explosions (initial total is zero), find the two velocities, and see they are opposite.
开一枪，肩膀会被枪托顶一下；火箭往下喷火，自己却往上冲。它们原本都是静止的，总动量是 0。现在分裂成两半，动量总和必须还是 0——所以一部分往左飞，另一部分必须往右飞，而且两边 mv 正好抵消。这就是反冲和爆炸的共同底层逻辑：一开始没动，结束后两半的动量加起来还是 0。Fire a gun and the stock shoves your shoulder; a rocket blasts gas downward while shooting upward. That is the shared logic of recoil and explosions: start with nothing moving, and the two halves still add to zero afterwards.
反冲的方程与规律：The recoil equation and rules:
m₁v₁ + m₂v₂ = 0 （初动量为 0，末动量仍为 0）m₁v₁ + m₂v₂ = 0 (initial 0, so final 0)

  • 质量大的一侧速度小，质量小的一侧速度大（火箭：喷出的气轻而快，火箭重而慢）。The heavier part is slower, the lighter part faster (a rocket: light fast gas, heavy slow rocket).

1 功的定义：力 × 位移Work: force × distance Work功
这一站要你会：说出功的定义 W = Fd（力 × 沿力方向的位移），单位焦耳 J，并会算「力斜着推」时只取沿位移方向的分力。Goal: state the definition of work, W = Fd (force × distance moved in its direction), in joules, and handle a force acting at an angle.
「做功」这词，物理老师管得特别严——不是你搬砖累到怀疑人生就叫做了功。功只认两件事：你使了多大的力，物体沿这个力的方向挪了多远。你死命顶着一堵墙，墙纹丝不动，那你在物理上「没做功」，因为位移是 0。反过来，10 N 的力把箱子推了 2 m，就是 20 J 的功。记住：力在、位移在、方向还对得上，才算功。"Work" is a strict word in physics — it is not the same as "tired". Work only cares about two things: how hard you push, and how far the object moves along that push. Shove a wall all day and it does not budge — physically you have done no work, because the displacement is zero. Push a box 2 m with a 10 N force and you have done 20 J of work. Force present, distance present, directions matching — that is work.
功的要点：Key points on work:
W = F × d
  • 单位焦耳 J：1 J = 1 N m，即 1 牛顿的力推 1 米做的功。Unit: the joule J — 1 J = 1 N m, the work done by 1 newton over 1 metre.
  • 力与位移有夹角 θ：只算沿位移方向的分力，W = F d cos θ。θ = 0 时 W = Fd；θ = 90° 时 W = 0（力白使了）。Force at angle θ: only the component along the motion counts, W = F d cos θ. When θ = 0, W = Fd; when θ = 90°, W = 0 (the force achieves nothing).
  • 功是标量（只有大小没有方向），可正可负：力帮助运动做正功，阻碍运动做负功。Work is a scalar (magnitude only), and can be positive or negative: a force helping the motion does positive work, one opposing it does negative work.
最容易混的坑：提着箱子水平走，人对箱子做的功是 0——因为拉力竖直向上，而位移是水平的，两者垂直，力在位移方向上的分量为 0。你觉得累，是因为肌肉在身体内部做功，不是物理上对箱子做功。The classic trap: carry a box horizontally and the work you do on it is zero — the lifting force is vertical, the motion is horizontal, so the component along the motion is zero. You feel tired because your muscles work inside your body, not because physics says you work on the box.
🗣 考场英文怎么说：How to say it in the exam: Work is done when a force moves its point of application in the direction of the force. Work = force × distance moved in the direction of the force, measured in joules (J), where 1 J = 1 N m.

2 动能：运动中的能量Kinetic energy: energy of motion Kinetic energy动能

That is why high speed is frightening — a little extra speed multiplies the crash energy several times.
动能的要点：Key points on kinetic energy:
KE = ½mv² W = ΔKE = ½mv² − ½mu²

3 重力势能：储存起来的功Gravitational potential energy: stored work GPE重力势能
这一站要你会：写出重力势能 GPE = mgh，理解「高度越高势能越大」，并知道势能是「储存起来的功」，落下时会转化为动能。Goal: write GPE = mgh, understand that higher up means more potential energy, and see potential energy as stored work that turns into kinetic energy when it falls.
把一本书举到书架顶上，你的胳膊做了功。这功哪去了？没消失——它被「存」进了书的高度，随时能取出来。一松手，书往下掉、越掉越快，存的势能就一点点变成动能。势能就是能量世界的「仓库」：把功先存起来，需要时再放出来。存多少，就看质量 m、重力加速度 g、高度 h 三个数相乘。Lift a book onto a high shelf and your arm has done work. Where did it go? It did not vanish — it got stored in the book's height, ready to be released. Let go and the book falls, faster and faster, as the stored energy turns into kinetic energy. Potential energy is the warehouse of the energy world: store work now, release it later. How much is stored is just mass m times g times height h.
重力势能的要点：Key points on gravitational potential energy:
GPE = mgh
  • GPE = mgh：m 质量（kg）、g = 9.8 m s⁻²、h 高度（m），单位焦耳 J。GPE = mgh: m in kg, g = 9.8 m s⁻², h in m, energy in joules.
  • 势能是「储存起来的功」：把物体竖直举高 h 做的功，正好等于 mgh。It is stored work: the work done to lift an object vertically through h is exactly mgh.
  • 高度是相对的：通常取地面（或题给的最低点）为 h = 0 的基准面。Height is relative: we usually take the ground (or the lowest point given) as the reference level where h = 0.

Pushing up a ramp, h counts only how far you climbed vertically, not how long the ramp is — this becomes very useful in stop 6.
🗣 考场英文怎么说：How to say it in the exam: Gravitational potential energy is the energy a body stores because of its position in a gravitational field. Near the Earth's surface it is given by GPE = mgh, where h is the vertical height above a chosen reference level.

4 能量守恒：能量只是搬家Conservation of energy: energy just moves Conservation能量守恒
这一站要你会：说出机械能守恒的条件（无摩擦/无阻力），会写 KE + PE = 常数，并能用 mgh = ½mv² 直接求落地速度。Goal: state the condition for conservation of mechanical energy (no friction/resistance), write KE + PE = constant, and use mgh = ½mv² to find the landing speed directly.
能量守恒是物理里最漂亮的一条定律，翻成大白话：能量不会凭空冒出来，也不会凭空消失，它只是换个「罐子」装。球从高处掉下来，势能罐里的能量一点点倒进动能罐；到地面时动能罐正好装满，和原来势能罐里的量一模一样。所以只问落地速度，根本不用管中间怎么掉——mgh 全变成 ½mv²，一解就出 v。Conservation of energy is one of the most beautiful laws in physics. In plain words: energy is never created or destroyed, it just moves to a different jar. As a ball falls, energy pours from the potential jar into the kinetic jar; by the ground the kinetic jar is exactly as full as the potential jar was. So to find the landing speed you never need to know how it fell — mgh all becomes ½mv², and one step gives v.
能量守恒的要点：Key points on conservation of energy:
KE + PE = constant mgh = ½mv² → v = √(2gh)
  • 机械能守恒（无摩擦、无空气阻力时）：KE + PE = 常数，即 ½mv² + mgh = 常量。Conservation (no friction, no air resistance): KE + PE = constant, i.e. ½mv² + mgh = constant.
  • 落地速度：从高度 h 由静止落下，mgh = ½mv² → v = √(2gh)。Landing speed: falling from rest through height h, mgh = ½mv² → v = √(2gh).
  • 有摩擦时：机械能不再守恒，减少的机械能 = 产生的热量（摩擦力做的负功）。With friction: mechanical energy is no longer conserved; the lost mechanical energy equals the heat produced (the negative work of friction).
有意思的发现：守恒式两边的 m 会约掉——所以自由落体的落地速度与质量无关！铁球和羽毛（忽略空气）从同一高度落下，落地速度一样快。A neat discovery: the m cancels from both sides — so the landing speed is independent of mass! A ball of iron and a feather (ignoring air) dropped from the same height hit the ground at the same speed.
🗣 考场英文怎么说：How to say it in the exam: When there is no friction or air resistance, the total mechanical energy (kinetic plus potential) is conserved. Energy is transferred between kinetic and potential forms, but the total never changes. With friction, some mechanical energy is converted to heat.
例题Example

① 顶端只有势能：PE = mgh；落地时只有动能：KE = ½mv²。① at the top only PE: mgh; at the ground only KE: ½mv². ② 能量守恒：mgh = ½mv²。② conservation: mgh = ½mv². ③ 约掉 m → gh = ½v² → v² = 2gh = 2 × 9.8 × 5 = 98 → v = √98 ≈ 9.9 m s⁻¹。③ cancel m → gh = ½v² → v² = 2gh = 2 × 9.8 × 5 = 98 → v = √98 ≈ 9.9 m s⁻¹.

5 功率：干活的快慢Power: how fast work is done Power功率
这一站要你会：写出功率的定义 P = 功 ÷ 时间，单位瓦特 W，并会用匀速时的 P = Fv 解题。Goal: write the definition of power, P = work ÷ time, in watts, and use P = Fv for constant-speed motion.
「功」告诉你干了多少活，「功率」告诉你干得有多快。同样搬 100 块砖，甲花 1 小时，乙花 2 小时，两人做的功一样，甲的功率却是乙的两倍。功率 = 功 ÷ 时间。还有个特别顺手的推论：机器匀速干活时，功率 = 力 × 速度（P = Fv）——起重机吊重物、汽车爬坡、电梯载人，全都能直接用力乘速度。Work tells you how much you did; power tells you how fast you did it. Two workers move 100 bricks, one in an hour and one in two hours — same work, but the first has twice the power. Power = work ÷ time. And a handy consequence: when a machine works at constant speed, power = force × velocity (P = Fv) — so cranes lifting, cars climbing, lifts carrying people all come down to force times speed.
功率的要点：Key points on power:
P = W / t P = Fv
  • P = W/t：功率 = 做功 ÷ 时间，单位瓦特 W（1 W = 1 J s⁻¹）。P = W/t: power = work ÷ time, measured in watts W (1 W = 1 J s⁻¹).
  • 匀速时 P = Fv：F 是力、v 是匀速运动的速度。推导：P = W/t = F×d/t = Fv。At constant speed P = Fv: F is the force, v the constant velocity. Derivation: P = W/t = F×d/t = Fv.
  • 功率大 = 干得快：同样多的功，功率越大，花的时间越短。More power = faster: for the same amount of work, more power means less time.
别混「平均」和「瞬时」：P = Fv 在匀速时最直接。变速时，P = Fv 给出的是「那一刻」的瞬时功率；若问平均功率，要用总功 ÷ 总时间。Don't mix average and instantaneous: P = Fv is simplest at constant speed. For changing speed, P = Fv gives the instantaneous power at that moment; for average power, use total work ÷ total time.
🗣 考场英文怎么说：How to say it in the exam: Power is the rate at which work is done, given by P = W/t and measured in watts (W), where 1 W = 1 J s⁻¹. When a force moves its point of application at constant velocity, the power is P = Fv.

6 能量法：解题的另一条捷径Energy methods: another shortcut Energy methods能量法
这一站要你会：在斜面、竖直运动等题里，用能量守恒（而不是 F=ma）一步求出速度，并能说出能量法比牛顿法快在哪。Goal: use conservation of energy (not F=ma) to find speeds in slope and vertical problems in one step, and explain when the energy method beats Newton's method.
到这一站，你手里有两套工具了：牛顿法（受力分析 + F=ma，第 4 单元）和能量法（起点能量 = 终点能量）。什么时候用能量法？记住一句话：题目只问「速度变得多快」，不问你「中间每一刻怎么动」时，能量法就是最快的路。它把一整段弯弯绕绕的过程压成一个等式——斜面多陡、拐了几个弯，统统不用管，起终点高度定了，速度就定了。By this stop you have two tools: the Newton way (forces + F=ma, unit 4) and the energy way (start energy = end energy). When to use energy? Remember: if the question only asks "how fast does it get", not "how does it move at every instant", energy is the fastest route. It squeezes a whole winding journey into one equation — the steepness of the slope and every bend stop mattering; fix the heights and the speed is fixed.
能量法的要点：Key points on the energy method:
KE + PE = constant mgh = ½mv² → v = √(2gh)
  • 能量法三步：① 写起点能量 ② 写终点能量 ③ 守恒式联立求解。Three steps: ① energy at the start, ② energy at the end, ③ equate and solve.
  • 适用题：斜面滑下、竖直下落、过山车、摆球——凡是「只关心起终点高度/速度」的题。When to use it: sliding down a slope, vertical falls, roller coasters, pendulums — anything where only the start/end heights and speeds matter.
  • 牛顿法 vs 能量法：牛顿法要知道每时每刻的力（过程信息全）；能量法只看起终点（过程可忽略）。选对方法，题目少算一半。Newton vs energy: Newton needs the forces at every instant (full journey detail); energy only looks at the endpoints. Pick the right one and the question is half-solved.
有摩擦别硬来：有摩擦时不能直接「机械能守恒」，要在方程里加摩擦生热：起点能量 = 终点能量 + 摩擦力做的功。漏掉这一项，答案一定错。Don't force it with friction: with friction you cannot use plain mechanical-energy conservation; add the heat produced: start energy = end energy + work done by friction. Miss that term and the answer is wrong.
🗣 考场英文怎么说：How to say it in the exam: When a question only asks for the final speed and not how the motion happens, use conservation of energy: write the energy at the start and at the end, then equate them. For a body falling from height h, mgh = ½mv² gives the speed directly.

单元 6 小结Unit 6 summary

① 功 W = Fd 是能量的「搬家公司」——力 × 沿力方向的位移，单位焦耳。
② 动能 KE = ½mv² 和重力势能 GPE = mgh，是能量的两个「储钱罐」。
③ 能量守恒：无摩擦时 KE + PE 不变；有摩擦时减少的机械能变成热。
④ 功率 P = W/t = Fv（匀速）衡量干活的快慢，单位瓦特。
⑤ 遇到「只问速度、不问过程」的题，用能量法——起点能量 = 终点能量，一步到位。① Work W = Fd moves energy around — force × distance moved in its direction, in joules.
② Kinetic energy KE = ½mv² and GPE = mgh are the two "piggy banks" of energy.
③ Conservation: with no friction KE + PE is constant; with friction the lost mechanical energy becomes heat.
④ Power P = W/t = Fv measures how fast work is done, in watts.
⑤ For "speed only, not the journey" questions, use the energy method — start energy = end energy.

这套「功与能」的语言，和前面第 4 单元的牛顿定律是一体两面：牛顿法看「每一刻的力」，能量法看「起点和终点」。两条路都能通，会用哪条用哪条。Work and energy are the flip side of Newton's laws in unit 4: the Newton way looks at forces at every instant, the energy way looks only at start and end. Both routes lead to the answer — use whichever is faster.
